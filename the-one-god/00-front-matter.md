# Overview

<a id="p1"></a>The One God provides a new understanding of the nature and reality of God and God’s plan and purpose in the world and in the Greater Community of life in the universe. It provides new answers to the deep and mystifying questions people have asked for millennia about God, the origin of life, creation, redemption, the soul and much more.

<a id="p2"></a>This book of the New Message gives us a new vantage point from which we can glimpse into the heart of God, back to the origins of the universe and come to understand the great separation from God that gave rise to the evolution of all life. It calls us down through the ancient corridors of our minds to the spiritual presence and power within us and provides the reasons we are each present in the world at this time.

<a id="p3"></a>The One God is the second book of Volume 1 of The New Message.
