# Overview

<a id="p1"></a>Living The Way of Knowledge is the New Message teaching on how to bring the grace, guidance and power of Knowledge into the Four Pillars of your life: The Pillar of Relationships, The Pillar of Work, The Pillar of Health and The Pillar of Spiritual Development. Like the four legs of a table, the Four Pillars provide a stable foundation for building a life in an unstable and uncertain world.

<a id="p2"></a>By building the Four Pillars of your life, you develop a foundation of greater certainty and direction in your life. It is the wisdom in Living The Way of Knowledge that provides the day-to-day insight needed on the journey of discovering and following Knowledge in a world undergoing radical environmental, political and social upheaval and emerging into a universe of intelligent life.

<a id="p3"></a>Living The Way of Knowledge is the third book of Volume 3 of The New Message.
