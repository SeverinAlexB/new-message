# Overview

<a id="p1"></a>The New Messenger is a book regarding the origin, lineage and mission of the Messengers of God who at different times in human history enter the world to receive and present new Revelations for humanity. This book reveals that all former Messengers are part of an Angelic assembly who oversee the world. This assembly has throughout human history and at the will of God sent one of their own into the world at specific times and places to serve the growing needs of humanity.

<a id="p2"></a>The New Messenger reveals that the entirety of this new Revelation is being given through one man: Marshall Vian Summers. People may ask: Who is he? Why was he chosen? How can you know he is real and authentic? And how is he connected to the Messengers of the past? Within the chapters of this book, people have an opportunity to learn who Marshall is, why he is here and what he is giving as the recipient of over 9000 pages of Revelation.

<a id="p3"></a>The New Messenger is the third book of Volume 1 of The New Message.
