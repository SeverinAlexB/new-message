# Overview

<a id="p1"></a>Our world is emerging into a Greater Community of intelligent life in the universe. Contact has begun and our isolation in the universe is over. This is the greatest evolutionary threshold in human history and yet we are mostly unaware and unprepared.

<a id="p2"></a>The Greater Community reveals:

<a id="p3"></a>- Who is visiting our world, why they are here and what they want

<a id="p4"></a>- The alien agenda and how it is affecting people everywhere

<a id="p5"></a>- Who the real allies of humanity are and how they are different from those forces intervening in our world

<a id="p6"></a>- The relationship between our core spirituality and contact with other forms of intelligent life

<a id="p7"></a>- How to prepare for the reality of Contact using the innate power that lives within us.

<a id="p8"></a>People around the world know at a deep level that extraterrestrial life is a reality and that we are not alone in the universe or even within our own world. This book provides a larger panorama and perspective on the reality of Contact with intelligent life in the universe, the need to prepare for this reality and the meaning of your life at this critical turning point in human history.

<a id="p9"></a>The Greater Community is the fourth book of Volume 1 of The New Message.
