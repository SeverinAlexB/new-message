# Overview

<a id="p1"></a>Preparing for the Great Waves of Change is a book revealed by Marshall Vian Summers to help us navigate the immense environmental, economic, and social changes now reshaping our world. This book delivers a powerful warning about the rapid depletion and degradation of the Earth’s resources, climate, and environment while calling for urgent action to restore the world, and adapt to and prepare for these growing new world conditions. It also discloses the reality of humanity’s place in a universe full of intelligent life, some of which is actively influencing our world and compounding our problems.

<a id="p2"></a>Through its teachings, Preparing for the Great Waves of Change challenges us to take responsibility—both individually and collectively—to navigate these turbulent times, restore balance to our planet, and chart a course toward a livable future for all of humanity. It unveils the deeper forces driving these changes while offering a pathway of resilience, clarity, and strength in uncertain times.

<a id="p3"></a>As humanity reaches a critical tipping point after centuries of overusing and exploiting the world’s resources and facing life from beyond our world, this book deepens our understanding of our shared destiny and the crucial choices that will determine the future of life on Earth.

<a id="p4"></a>Preparing for the Great Waves of Change is a book of Volume 2 of The New Message.
