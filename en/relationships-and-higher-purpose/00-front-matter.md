# Overview

<a id="p1"></a>Relationships and Higher Purpose takes you beyond the normal parameters of relationships to an experience where deeper union, purpose and meaning with others are possible. It expands the context of relationships to include a relationship with Knowledge (What is Knowledge?), with your Spiritual Family (Your Relationship with your Spiritual Family) and with the forces that are shaping your life and the world.

<a id="p2"></a>Relationships and Higher Purpose helps people everywhere find and fulfill a life of purpose, meaning and destiny in a world undergoing environmental, political and social upheaval while also facing Contact with other intelligent life.

<a id="p3"></a>Relationships and Higher Purpose is a book of Volume 4 of The New Message.
