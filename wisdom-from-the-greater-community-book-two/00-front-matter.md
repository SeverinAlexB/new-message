# Overview

<a id="p1"></a>Wisdom from The Greater Community Book Two concentrates on the needs of people living in a world in transition as the context for finding purpose and resolving personal dilemmas. It speaks of the larger arena of intelligent life called the Greater Community and provides insights and perspectives necessary to prepare for environmental, political and social upheaval and to understand our vulnerabilities as we emerge into a universe of intelligent life.

<a id="p2"></a>Wisdom from The Greater Community Book Two delivers topics ranging from “Ambition” and “Solving Problems” to “Work” and “Greater Community Realities,” It opens a path for living a meaningful life in the world. Both books of Wisdom from the Greater Community are powerful complements to the daily spiritual practice offered in Steps to Knowledge.

<a id="p3"></a>Wisdom from the Greater Community Book Two is the second book of Volume 6 of The New Message.
