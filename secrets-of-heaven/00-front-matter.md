# Overview

<a id="p1"></a>From the depths of the unknown comes the book Secrets of Heaven to take you deep into the mystery and purpose surrounding your presence in the world.

<a id="p2"></a>The Angelic assembly shares what has long been forgotten — truths that once guided the prophets, poets, Messengers and mystics throughout the ages. The ancient memory and awareness that dwell within every person can be rekindled to reveal your gifts and purpose in life. The Secrets of Heaven holds the power to unlock gifts deep at the heart of humanity bringing new hope into the world.

<a id="p3"></a>Secrets of Heaven is the first book of [Volume 7](https://newmessage.org/the-message/volume-7) of The New Message.
