# Overview

<a id="p1"></a>The Great Waves of Change presents a picture of a radically changing planet and the steps we can take to navigate this increasingly turbulent and uncertain world. This book presents a revolutionary new way of gaining certainty and direction in life, a unique process that people everywhere can apply. By understanding the Great Waves of environmental, political, and social disruption and connecting to Knowledge, the deeper authority within, people can find the strength and inner certainty to adapt and become contributors in the world.

<a id="p2"></a>The Great Waves of Change is an essential resource you can utilize to mentally, emotionally and practically prepare yourself for the difficult times ahead. By preparing for and contributing to the world, you can develop the strength and resilience to face the environmental, political, and social upheaval ahead and also begin to understand our vulnerability in a universe full of intelligent life.

<a id="p3"></a>The Great Waves of Change is a book within Volume 5 of The New Message.
