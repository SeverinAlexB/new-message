# Greater Community Spirituality: A New Revelation — Index

A chaptered Markdown conversion of Marshall Vian Summers' *Greater Community Spirituality: A New Revelation*. This folder follows the same source-file conventions as the Allies corpus: YAML frontmatter, one Markdown file per section, and paragraph anchors for stable references.

See also: [../index.md](../index.md) for the Allies of Humanity master index.

## Chapters (TOC + summaries)

- **`00-front-matter.md`** — Front Matter (New Message Books, Preface, Introduction)
  - Summary: Collects the title, publication, dedication, New Message book list, Preface, and Introduction. Summers frames Greater Community Spirituality as a revealed teaching for humanity's emergence into a larger universe, emphasizing Knowledge as the translatable core of spiritual experience.
  - Key concepts: Greater Community Spirituality, New Message, Knowledge, Preface, Introduction

- **`01-what-is-god.md`** — Chapter 1: What is God?
  - Summary: Defines God less as an object of belief than as the experience of total relationship and Knowledge. The chapter distinguishes a local, human conception of God from a Greater Community understanding that can be recognized across worlds and cultures.
  - Key concepts: God, Knowledge, relationship, Greater Community, spirituality, human purpose

- **`02-what-is-the-world.md`** — Chapter 2: What is the World?
  - Summary: Presents the world as a place of learning, contribution, and preparation rather than a possession or final home. It situates human life within a larger evolutionary and spiritual context.
  - Key concepts: world, Greater Community, learning, environment, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`03-what-is-life-force.md`** — Chapter 3: What is Life Force?
  - Summary: Explains Life Force as a real but limited power operating in the mental environment. The chapter warns that power without Knowledge can mislead, making discernment essential.
  - Key concepts: Life Force, Mental Environment, power, discernment, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`04-what-is-knowledge.md`** — Chapter 4: What is Knowledge?
  - Summary: Introduces Knowledge as the deeper spiritual intelligence within the individual. It contrasts Knowledge with belief, fear, preference, and social conditioning.
  - Key concepts: Knowledge, inner guidance, spiritual intelligence, purpose, Greater Community, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`05-what-is-human-purpose.md`** — Chapter 5: What is Human Purpose?
  - Summary: Frames human purpose as contribution discovered through Knowledge and relationship. Purpose is presented as something lived and revealed through service rather than invented by the personal mind, and introduces the idea of bringing gifts from one's Ancient home into the world before returning to one's Spiritual Family.
  - Key concepts: human purpose, contribution, relationship, service, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality

- **`06-what-is-the-greater-community.md`** — Chapter 6: What is the Greater Community?
  - Summary: Defines the Greater Community as the larger arena of intelligent life into which humanity is emerging. It stresses that this context changes the meaning of spirituality, preparation, and human destiny.
  - Key concepts: Greater Community, intelligent life, human emergence, preparation, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`07-how-is-wisdom-achieved-in-life.md`** — Chapter 7: How is Wisdom achieved in life?
  - Summary: Describes Wisdom as something gained through experience, restraint, discernment, and preparation. Wisdom is treated as the practical counterpart to Knowledge.
  - Key concepts: Wisdom, experience, discernment, preparation, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`08-who-is-wisdom-meant-for.md`** — Chapter 8: Who is Wisdom meant for?
  - Summary: Clarifies that Wisdom is meant for those who are ready to undertake preparation and service. The chapter emphasizes maturity, humility, and the responsibilities that come with greater understanding.
  - Key concepts: Wisdom, studentship, readiness, service, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`09-how-does-god-participate-in-the-world.md`** — Chapter 9: How does God participate in the world?
  - Summary: Explains Divine participation as working through Knowledge, relationship, and unseen assistance rather than direct control of worldly events. It presents God as active through deeper recognition and calling.
  - Key concepts: God, Unseen Ones, Divine participation, Knowledge, Greater Community, spirituality, human purpose

- **`10-how-do-you-prepare.md`** — Chapter 10: How do you prepare?
  - Summary: Describes preparation as a disciplined return to Knowledge rather than accumulation of ideas. It emphasizes practice, patience, and willingness to be remade by a greater purpose.
  - Key concepts: preparation, practice, Knowledge, discipline, Greater Community, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`11-what-is-your-preparation-for.md`** — Chapter 11: What is your preparation for?
  - Summary: Explains that preparation equips a person for contribution, relationship, and participation in a Greater Community reality. It links personal development to service beyond personal ambition.
  - Key concepts: preparation, purpose, service, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`12-whom-will-you-meet.md`** — Chapter 12: Whom will you meet?
  - Summary: Discusses the people and relationships one may encounter in preparation. It emphasizes recognition, companionship, true teachers and allies, and the deeper relationships with one's Spiritual Family that stand behind one's life in the world.
  - Key concepts: relationship, companions, teachers, recognition, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`13-what-must-be-unlearned.md`** — Chapter 13: What must be unlearned?
  - Summary: Identifies beliefs, assumptions, self-images, and inherited conditioning that must be released. Unlearning is presented as necessary for Knowledge to become clear and active.
  - Key concepts: unlearning, belief, assumptions, freedom, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`14-what-must-be-avoided.md`** — Chapter 14: What must be avoided?
  - Summary: Warns against errors, distractions, false power, and forms of spiritual ambition that can derail preparation. The chapter stresses sobriety and discernment.
  - Key concepts: avoidance, discernment, error, spiritual practice, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`15-who-serves-humanity.md`** — Chapter 15: Who serves humanity?
  - Summary: Explains who truly serves humanity from within and beyond the world. This chapter connects Greater Community Spirituality to the Allies of Humanity, the Unseen Ones, the Harim, the Wise, and hidden networks of assistance that preserve Knowledge across worlds.
  - Key concepts: Allies of Humanity, service, Unseen Ones, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`16-who-are-the-unseen-ones.md`** — Chapter 16: Who are the Unseen Ones?
  - Summary: Describes the Unseen Ones as a spiritual presence serving the development of Knowledge in intelligent life. It frames their work as guidance without domination or spectacle.
  - Key concepts: Unseen Ones, Angelic Presence, guidance, service, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`17-how-does-revelation-occur-in-human-life.md`** — Chapter 17: How does revelation occur in human life?
  - Summary: Explores revelation as an event and process in human life. It distinguishes authentic revelation from personal fantasy or social belief, links it to Knowledge and spiritual authority, and warns that premature contact with one's Spiritual Family or Ancient home can distract from service in the world.
  - Key concepts: revelation, Knowledge, Messenger, spiritual authority, Greater Community, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`18-what-is-human-destiny.md`** — Chapter 18: What is human destiny?
  - Summary: Places human destiny within emergence into the Greater Community. The chapter presents destiny as both collective and individual, requiring preparation, unity, and contribution.
  - Key concepts: human destiny, Greater Community, evolution, purpose, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`19-what-is-religion.md`** — Chapter 19: What is religion?
  - Summary: Defines religion as a vehicle for spiritual experience rather than an end in itself. It distinguishes living Knowledge from institutional identity and inherited belief, and states that the world and one's Ancient home are different realities whose relationship must be mediated through contribution.
  - Key concepts: religion, spirituality, Knowledge, tradition, Greater Community, God, human purpose

- **`20-what-is-religious-education-and-who-is-it-for.md`** — Chapter 20: What is religious education and who is it for?
  - Summary: Explains religious education as preparation for those called to deeper participation. It emphasizes readiness, practice, and the practical cultivation of Knowledge and Wisdom.
  - Key concepts: religious education, studentship, preparation, teaching, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`21-what-is-the-greater-community-way-of-knowledge.md`** — Chapter 21: What is The Greater Community Way of Knowledge?
  - Summary: Introduces The Greater Community Way of Knowledge as a path of preparation suited to life in a larger universe. It emphasizes universality, practice, and direct inner knowing.
  - Key concepts: Greater Community Way of Knowledge, Knowledge, practice, preparation, Greater Community, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`22-where-can-knowledge-be-found.md`** — Chapter 22: Where can Knowledge be found?
  - Summary: Answers where Knowledge can be found by pointing to the deeper interior life and the relationships that activate it. Discovery requires stillness, honesty, and preparation.
  - Key concepts: Knowledge, inner life, discovery, practice, Greater Community, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`23-how-is-knowledge-translated-in-the-greater-community.md`** — Chapter 23: How is Knowledge translated in the Greater Community?
  - Summary: Discusses how Knowledge can be translated across worlds, cultures, and forms of intelligence. It frames Knowledge as the universal spiritual medium beneath language and custom.
  - Key concepts: Knowledge, translation, Greater Community, communication, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`24-what-is-steps-to-knowledge.md`** — Chapter 24: What is Steps to Knowledge?
  - Summary: Explains Steps to Knowledge as a structured preparation for reclaiming Knowledge. The chapter presents practice as the bridge between spiritual promise and lived ability.
  - Key concepts: Steps to Knowledge, practice, preparation, studentship, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`25-what-is-grace.md`** — Chapter 25: What is Grace?
  - Summary: Defines Grace as the presence and movement of the Divine through Knowledge and relationship. It treats Grace as practical assistance and recognition, not merely sentiment.
  - Key concepts: Grace, Knowledge, Divine, relationship, Greater Community, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`26-who-is-jesus.md`** — Chapter 26: Who is Jesus?
  - Summary: Reconsiders Jesus within a Greater Community context. The chapter distinguishes the living reality of a Messenger from religious projection and cultural possession.
  - Key concepts: Jesus, Messenger, religion, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`27-what-is-the-evolution-of-religion-in-the-world.md`** — Chapter 27: What is the evolution of religion in the world?
  - Summary: Describes the evolution of religion as humanity enters a larger arena of life. It argues that religion must become more universal, Knowledge-based, and capable of translation beyond the human world.
  - Key concepts: religion, evolution, New Revelation, Greater Community, Knowledge, God, spirituality, human purpose

- **`28-back-matter.md`** — Back Matter (About Marshall Vian Summers, About the Society, Join the Preparation)
  - Summary: Collects biographical and organizational back matter about Marshall Vian Summers, the Society for the New Message, and the invitation to continue preparation.
  - Key concepts: Marshall Vian Summers, Society for the New Message, New Message, Steps to Knowledge, preparation
