# Glossary — The Allies of Humanity

Definitions of the specialized terminology used across the four books. Where a term is defined in more than one back-matter and the wording differs, the canonical (most complete) definition is given first, followed by per-book variations noted in italics. Source refs use the form **B`<n>`/`<chapter-prefix>`** (e.g. **B1/08** = Book 1's `08-back-matter.md`). Book 4's back-matter contains no glossary section; entries marked with B4 reference recurring usage in the wider book rather than a back-matter definition.

## Terms (A–Z)

### Allies of Humanity
A group of observers from a community of free, ethically-mature races in nearby space who positioned themselves discreetly in our solar system to watch the alien presence on Earth and to relay back what they saw. Their role is strictly advisory: they will not intervene on our behalf but instead pass on perspective, warning and wisdom so that humanity can defend itself. They are presented as representatives of "the Wise" who exist throughout the Greater Community.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07
*Variations*: **B1** stresses their hidden vantage point in our solar system and their reporting/advisory mission. **B2** describes them in past tense ("were hidden"), implying the observation phase has concluded. **B3** frames them as a community of free races calling humanity to build sovereignty, unity and freedom.

### Collectives
Large, hierarchical alliances made up of several alien races bound together by a shared allegiance and common command structure. More than one Collective is currently active on Earth, and these competing groups are pursuing overlapping but rival agendas. They are non-military commercial-style organizations seeking biological and material resources from our world, and they do not value human freedom.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07
*Variations*: **B1** and **B2** explicitly note the competing agendas; **B3** drops the "competing agendas" phrase but otherwise matches.

### Contact
The historical threshold at which a young race encounters intelligent life from beyond its world. In the Briefings it is described as the most consequential turning point humanity has ever faced — already underway, and capable of determining the species' future for generations. The books distinguish ethical Contact (information shared from afar, no interference) from the Intervention (uninvited presence pursuing its own agenda).

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07

### Declaration of Human Sovereignty
A reader-drafted document — modeled on the U.S. Declaration of Independence — that asserts humanity's rights as the indigenous people of Earth, sets out an ethics of Contact, and proposes Rules of Engagement for any future visitation. It is meant to be circulated globally so that other races aware of Earth understand we intend to determine our own future.

*Source*: B1/08

### Earth-Based Solutions
The principle, emphasized across all four books, that every problem facing humanity can be solved with resources, intelligence and cooperation native to Earth — and that what we lack is not technology but unity, will and cooperation. Reliance on ET technology offered by the Intervention is presented as the path to dependency and loss of freedom.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07

### Four Arenas of the Intervention
The Briefings' summary of how the Intervention operates: (1) co-opting individuals in positions of power, commerce and religion through promises of wealth, power or technology; (2) building hidden establishments from which to project mental influence and run the Pacification Program; (3) manipulating humanity's religious values and spiritual impulses to capture allegiance; (4) abducting people to support an interbreeding program designed to create a hybrid leadership.

*Source*: B2/10, B3/08, B4/07

### Greater Community
The vast physical and spiritual Universe — i.e., space itself — into which humanity is now emerging, populated by intelligent life in countless forms and at every stage of development. It is both an environment and a context: the larger arena of relationships, civilizations, rules and spiritual realities within which Earth must now learn to operate.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07
*Variations*: **B3** adds the phrase "stages of evolution," emphasizing developmental diversity.

### Greater Community Education
The body of preparation — the Allies Briefings together with the New Message teaching on Life in the Universe — described as the only education currently available on Earth for navigating life in a populated Universe.

*Source*: B3/08, B4/07

### Greater Community Spirituality
A spiritual teaching said to come from the Creator and practiced in many worlds across the Greater Community. It teaches how to recognize and live by Knowledge and how to safeguard individual freedom in a populated Universe. It has been delivered to Earth specifically to prepare humanity for the realities of life beyond our world. The book of the same name is described as a "Theology of Contact."

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08

### Greater Community Way of Knowledge (The Way of Knowledge)
A broader umbrella for the various traditions taught in many Greater Community worlds for awakening and following Knowledge and Wisdom. On Earth it is presented through Marshall Vian Summers' writings (Steps to Knowledge, Wisdom from the Greater Community, Living the Way of Knowledge, etc.) as the practical preparation for emergence.

*Source*: B1/08 (as "The Ways of Insight" / "Greater Community Way of Knowledge"), B2/10 (as "The Way of Knowledge"), B3/08 (as "The Way of Knowledge")
*Variations*: **B1** uses the older label "The Ways of Insight" for the cross-world traditions and "The Greater Community Way of Knowledge" for the Earth-bound teaching. **B2** and **B3** consolidate both under "The Way of Knowledge."

### Hidden Establishments
The other concealed footholds the Intervention is creating on Earth — bases or installations from which it projects influence into the Mental Environment and pursues the Pacification Program. One of the four declared arenas of intervention activity.

*Source*: B2/10, B3/08, B4/07

### Human Destiny
The idea that humanity is fated, by the natural course of its evolution, to step out of planetary isolation and enter the Greater Community of intelligent life. This emergence is treated as inevitable; what remains open is whether humanity arrives prepared and free, or unprepared and dominated.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07

### Human Sovereignty
Humanity's right and responsibility, as the native people of Earth, to determine who may enter our world, on what terms, and under what rules. Closely tied to the Declaration of Human Sovereignty and to the call for humans to set their own Rules of Engagement before any further Contact is accepted.

*Source*: B3/08, B4/07 (recurring concept; not in B1/B2 glossaries)

### Interbreeding Program
One of the four arenas of the Intervention: the covert taking of human beings — often without their awareness — to support a hybridization effort aimed at producing a hybrid race and a new leadership cadre psychologically and biologically bonded to the Visitors.

*Source*: B2/10, B3/08, B4/07

### Intervention
The collective presence, intent and ongoing activity of the alien Visitors in Earth's affairs. It is characterized by deception and persuasion rather than open force, and its goal is to take quiet control of Earth's resources and population by integrating itself into human institutions, leadership and biology.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07

### Knowledge
The deeper spiritual intelligence that lives within every person — the source of true knowing, intrinsic understanding and eternal wisdom. It is the timeless part of a being that cannot be manipulated, deceived or corrupted by any worldly or Greater Community power, and it exists as a latent potential in all intelligent life. The books describe it as God-within and as the only reliable defense against mental influence from advanced races.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07
*Variations*: **B1** and **B2** include the formula that Knowledge is God in you and God is all Knowledge in the Universe. **B3** drops the explicit God-formulation but keeps the rest.

### Marshall Vian Summers (MV Summers)
The recipient and messenger of both the Allies Briefings and the wider Greater Community / New Message teachings. Founder of The Society for the Greater Community Way of Knowledge (later The Society for the New Message), he is presented across the four books as the human channel through which this material has entered the world.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07

### Mental Environment
The invisible environment of thought, attention and influence in which everyone lives. It is described as more determinative of behavior than the physical environment, and it is a domain where stronger or more concentrated minds can shape, persuade or dominate weaker minds. The Intervention works heavily in this domain, as do governments, commerce and culture more broadly.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07
*Variations*: **B1** defines it broadly as "the environment of thought and mental influence." **B2** and **B3** sharpen the definition to highlight that more concentrated minds exert influence over weaker ones.

### New Message from God
The wider revelation, of which the Allies Briefings are one part, said to be delivered through Marshall Vian Summers from the Creator. It encompasses the teachings on Greater Community Spirituality, Steps to Knowledge, and Life in the Universe, providing both the theological and practical preparation for humanity's emergence.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07

### Pacification Program
The Visitors' coordinated program of mental persuasion and influence aimed at numbing humanity's awareness, dulling discernment of the Intervention, and producing a passive, compliant population. Operates primarily within the Mental Environment.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07

### Rules of Engagement
The boundaries, protocols and conditions that any free native race must establish for itself to govern Contact with outside powers — who may approach, when, on what terms, and for what purposes. Humanity is urged to set its own Rules of Engagement before any further visitation is permitted; the Intervention has bypassed this normal developmental step.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10 (referenced), B4/07

### Society for the Greater Community Way of Knowledge / Society for the New Message
The non-profit religious organization founded by Marshall Vian Summers to receive, present and disseminate the Greater Community / New Message teachings worldwide. Renamed The Society for the New Message in later books.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10 (as Society for the Greater Community Way of Knowledge); B3/08, B4/07 (as Society for the New Message)

### Steps to Knowledge
The book of practice in the Greater Community Way of Knowledge — a 365-step preparation guide for experiencing and applying Knowledge directly. Functions as the foundational practice manual referenced repeatedly across all four books and won the Year 2000 Book of the Year Award for Spirituality.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07

### Theology of Contact
A descriptor used for the framework formed by Greater Community Spirituality and Steps to Knowledge taken together — the first explicitly spiritual/theological account of what Contact with the Greater Community means and requires.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07

### Three Requirements of a Free Race
The conditions any free world must meet to remain free in the Greater Community, named explicitly in Book 4: unity, self-sufficiency and discretion. Humanity is urged to build all three before further Contact.

*Source*: B4/07

### Unseen Ones (a.k.a. Teachers / Teachers of the Greater Community / Angels of the Creator)
The angelic presences who oversee the spiritual development of sentient beings throughout the Greater Community. The Allies refer to them by this name; in the broader teaching they are also called the Teachers or Teachers of the Greater Community. They serve the emergence of Knowledge across many worlds without intervening directly in any one race's affairs.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08
*Variations*: **B1** identifies them only as "The Unseen Ones" and "Angels of the Creator." **B2** adds the synonym "Teachers of the Greater Community." **B3** uses simply "Teachers" as the alternate name.

### Visitors
The several alien races from the Greater Community who are present on Earth without our consent and are actively interfering in human life. They are engaged in a slow project of weaving themselves into our societies, leadership and biology in order to take control of the planet's resources and people.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07

### The Wise
A general designation, used across the Briefings, for those individuals and races throughout the Greater Community who live by Knowledge and have learned to remain free in a competitive Universe. The Allies are described as representing "the Wise in many worlds," and the wider teaching speaks of networks of such free races who keep their distance, protect their sovereignty, and pass wisdom to emerging worlds rather than intervening in them.

*Source*: B1/08, B2/10, B3/08, B4/07
