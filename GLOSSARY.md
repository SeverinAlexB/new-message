# Allies of Humanity Terminology

This is a source-backed navigation guide to recurring terms in the four current web editions. Definitions are brief paraphrases; follow the chapter links for the official wording and context.

## Allies of Humanity

The observers from free worlds who deliver the Briefings while declining to intervene or govern humanity. See [Book One, Final Words](en/AOHB1_book-1-allies-of-humanity/07-final-words.md) and [Book Four, First Briefing](en/AOHB4_book-4-freedom-in-the-universe/01-first-briefing-who-we-are-and-what-we-represent.md).

## Collectives

Hierarchical commercial powers composed of multiple races under a common authority, described as competitors for resources and influence. See [Book Two, Fourth Briefing](en/AOHB2_book-2-human-unity-and-freedom/04-fourth-briefing-the-collectives.md).

## Contact

Humanity's encounter with intelligent life beyond Earth. The Briefings distinguish responsible contact from covert intervention. See [Book Three, First Briefing](en/AOHB3_book-3-a-message-to-earth/01-first-briefing-the-reality-of-contact.md).

## Greater Community

The larger physical universe of intelligent life, nations, trade and competing powers into which humanity is emerging. See [Book Two, First Briefing](en/AOHB2_book-2-human-unity-and-freedom/01-first-briefing-the-universe-into-which-you-are-emerging.md).

## Intervention

The covert effort described in the Briefings to gain access to Earth's resources and human allegiance through persuasion, influence and dependency rather than open conquest. See [Book One, First Briefing](en/AOHB1_book-1-allies-of-humanity/01-first-briefing-the-extraterrestrial-presence-in-the-world-today.md) and [Book Two, Third Commentary](en/AOHB2_book-2-human-unity-and-freedom/09-third-commentary-understanding-the-intervention.md).

## Knowledge

The deeper spiritual intelligence within the individual, presented as the basis for discernment and freedom from manipulation. See [Book One, Third Briefing](en/AOHB1_book-1-allies-of-humanity/03-third-briefing-a-great-warning.md).

## Mental Environment

The environment of thought and influence in which minds can affect one another. The Briefings describe it as a central arena of the Intervention. See [Book Two, Third Briefing](en/AOHB2_book-2-human-unity-and-freedom/03-third-briefing-the-influence-upon-humanity.md).

## Networks of the Wise

Discreet networks of free peoples that preserve knowledge and limited communication without becoming visible political or commercial powers. See [Book Four, Sixth Briefing](en/AOHB4_book-4-freedom-in-the-universe/06-sixth-briefing-the-networks-of-the-wise.md).

## Pacification Program

The program of mental influence described as encouraging passivity, compliance and diminished discernment. See [Book Two, Second Commentary](en/AOHB2_book-2-human-unity-and-freedom/08-second-commentary-effects-of-the-pacification-program.md).

## Self-Sufficiency

One of the requirements for a free race: meeting essential needs without dependence on foreign powers. See [Book Three, Second Briefing](en/AOHB3_book-3-a-message-to-earth/02-second-briefing-the-requirements-for-freedom-in-the-universe.md).

## Unseen Ones

The non-physical spiritual presence that the Allies identify as guiding their mission while not controlling its outcome. See [Book One, Sixth Briefing](en/AOHB1_book-1-allies-of-humanity/06-sixth-briefing-questions-and-answers.md).

## Visitors

The extraterrestrial groups operating in the world as described by the Briefings, especially those participating in the Intervention. See [Book One, First Briefing](en/AOHB1_book-1-allies-of-humanity/01-first-briefing-the-extraterrestrial-presence-in-the-world-today.md).

## Way of Knowledge

The spiritual preparation repeatedly recommended as a means of developing inner certainty, discernment and responsible action. See [Book Two, Sixth Briefing](en/AOHB2_book-2-human-unity-and-freedom/06-sixth-briefing-a-call-to-action.md).
