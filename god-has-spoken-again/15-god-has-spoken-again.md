# God Has Spoken Again

<a id="p1"></a>*God Has Spoken Again *is the beginning of a new message for humanity. This book contains a warning, a blessing and a preparation for a new world reality and provides us with new answers to the growing crises of war, environmental destruction, religious conflict, human suffering and poverty in the world.

<a id="p2"></a>This first book of the New Message presents the goal and purpose of this Revelation and its essential communication to the individual at the level of our deeper spiritual intelligence. This opens a window into humanity’s future in the Greater Community of life and how this is connected to the reasons we are each present in the world at this time.

<a id="p3"></a>*God Has Spoken Again* is the first book of [Volume 1](https://www.newmessage.org/the-message/volume-1/) of The New Message.
