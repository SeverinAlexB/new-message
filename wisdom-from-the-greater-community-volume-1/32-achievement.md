# Chapter 32: Achievement

<a id="p1"></a>I would like to speak about achievement and relationship. Sometimes people are uncomfortable with the things I say. To a certain degree, that cannot be helped because not everyone likes everything. Sometimes I put a little pebble in your shoe, so to speak, because it is good to think about things. Often when people are engaged in discussing very important matters, it is very easy to maintain self-comforting ideas and rely upon them to answer all the controversies that may arise. To penetrate the real truth within your life, however, sometimes you must let these controversies rage a little and see what happens. Sometimes you must think very carefully about your own assumptions and be able to distinguish your assumptions from those things you really know to be true. In other words, you must distinguish between belief and direct experience.

<a id="p2"></a>Life is very intelligent. It is always conspiring to move you into a larger sphere of experience. A part of each person always wants to stay in a smaller sphere of experience, which is actually not experience at all. It is merely a realm of self-assuring ideas. So, sometimes it is necessary to sort of break things up a little, bring in some new ideas or say things that are challenging. That is healthy. There must always be trust that if something that is held very deeply is challenged, there is something within you that is really sound, true and stable. You are all trying to get to that because that is where all meaning and true direction come from.

<a id="p3"></a>When people experience life, it is always interpretive. In other words, what you see is interpretive. What you hear is interpretive. What you feel from within yourself is interpretive. However, the Source of Knowledge within you is not interpretive and the more you can set aside interpretation, the greater your direct experience of life will be. This will give you a vision of life that is quite profound and extremely useful. You will be able to perceive situations around you with greater clarity of mind. You will be able to see what is there rather than what you want to be there because your preferences are exerting less influence upon you. This makes your vision clearer and your presence more powerful.

<a id="p4"></a>There are many popular spiritual ideas that are very deeply cherished, and people very often think that these are not ideas but absolute truths themselves. But they are really ideas. Anything your mind settles on is an idea. What is most important is where true ideas come from. That is why we have often said, "Do not be content with ideas alone." Whether they be comforting or not, find out where they come from. That is the great opportunity.

<a id="p5"></a>You have all come here to achieve something. It is a possibility. There are no guarantees that you will do it in this life because that is up to you. You have all come to do something specific. By achieving that, you will be able to end separation within yourself and therefore be able to complete your work at this level. However, this thing you have come to do is not so obvious. You cannot say, "I know what it is already. It is to do this!" This is a very comforting idea, but not terribly helpful. If you sense that you have an achievement in life that you must find, it is important that there is someone who loves you enough to challenge you when that is necessary, to comfort you when that is necessary and to reassure you when that is necessary.

<a id="p6"></a>An achievement has two aspects: the first is a realization, the second is a physical accomplishment. You cannot separate them. They must happen together. Some people want the realization without the responsibility of the physical accomplishment. Many people want the physical accomplishment and just bypass the realization. "Just tell me what it is, Conte. I'll go do it tomorrow. Or maybe I'll do it tonight, if it is important. Just tell me what it is. Tell me what to do!" So, in my discourses I have given some very lofty goals to achieve, not so easy but worthwhile nonetheless. I want everyone here to aim very high in life, perhaps higher than you think you can go. That is good.

<a id="p7"></a>The realization is very important because it gives you the power of accomplishment. It also resolves many other issues that are very substantial in themselves. The physical accomplishment is very important because here you leave your mark upon the world. Then you achieve a satisfaction that very few people will achieve. You have completed something that is absolutely fundamental to your being here. There is even satisfaction in approaching the accomplishment and in all the various stages of accomplishment, for now you are not questioning your reality. You are not having to continually ask the same question, "What am I doing? What am I doing? What am I doing?" You are able to give yourself increasingly to a primary focus in life, and that in itself ends conflict.

<a id="p8"></a>There is something else very important about achievement: You must be extremely concentrated, not only concentrated in terms of your perspective, but concentrated in terms of your life force. You must have tremendous vitality to go in the direction that you are discerning. You will find this quality apparent in all individuals who are achieving something in any area--a tremendous concentration of their energy and their focus.

<a id="p9"></a>When we are training people to concentrate, people find out it is not so easy to do if you are not used to it. It can seem very self-limiting to concentrate like that, but it begins to generate spiritual power in your life and to harmonize your outer affairs. In fact, it requires that things be resolved, and so it begins to generate a tremendous build-up of force that is quite essential.

<a id="p10"></a>To achieve anything in this life you must exert tremendous energy to do it, and yet the achievement that we have often spoken of is not only achievement based upon your own ability, but it is carrying a Greater Power in your life that can far exceed anything that you individually can do. It cannot do this without your development as a vehicle and without your development as a partner. It is very exciting!

<a id="p11"></a>Now, few people will actually be the seed person for the accomplishment. Yet they will require a community of people to help them do whatever it is given them to do that they have realized, and everyone will be uplifted in doing that.

<a id="p12"></a>When you are concentrated, you have power. Whether it be for good or for ill, you have power. Those who are committed to destruction are very concentrated, yes? That is why they are so influential. If the general population is not concentrated, either individually or collectively, it is easily manipulated-sad but true.

<a id="p13"></a>Therefore, if you wish to make a contribution in life, it is very important to generate an increasing concentration on your power and development. This means pulling yourself in at certain times and directing your emphasis in only one or two areas. This magnifies your presence and power and brings about resolution. When you are truly engaged in achievement, you are concentrating on what is essential. What is essential is gaining your attention and involvement. This concentrates your life and, as I have said, with concentration comes the resolution of conflict. More and more of yourself is aiming in one direction. There is less and less division internally.

<a id="p14"></a>Our curriculum is for people who know they must carry on with achievement. Beyond even their personal preferences or desire for comfort, there is something now that is driving them, and they must proceed. Our curriculum is for these people.

<a id="p15"></a>We wish to illustrate a very honest and clear picture of what true preparation is and what it requires. Then people can become more honest with this issue of achievement. Now, in normal conversation people talk about great achievements that they want to do or intend to do or will do someday or should do, and so forth. The word "commitment" is used very lightly, and the word "dedication" is used very lightly. These things deserve reverence. Indeed they do. When you have a taste of what it really means to be committed or to be devoted or to be innerdirected or to give yourself completely to something, then you will see the difference and how important it is.

<a id="p16"></a>Somehow you must find your place in life and you must find a way to gain access to that part of you that has come here to achieve something. It is not true that you are here merely to make up for past mistakes. It is not true that you are here merely to resolve personal dilemmas. That is not true! That is a very limited viewpoint. It does not hold respect for your origin, your Source or your true Community beyond this world.

<a id="p17"></a>Often we have spoken of Spiritual Family, and I would like to say a few words about this because many people who have heard this are not yet certain what I mean. In life thus far you have had a physical family, a family of the world that has made it possible for you to be born, to be raised and to be educated in the ways of the world. Likewise, you have a Spiritual Family that is waiting to prepare you, to activate your spiritual life and your Knowledge while you are here in the world. All sparks of achievement come from beyond the world. The achievement may have a very physical manifestation. It may have a very mundane application, but the inspiration for it has come from beyond the world-all achievement for good, that is.

<a id="p18"></a>Therefore, you have a physical family, a worldly family, that has raised you from birth and has educated you to prepare you to be in life. Maybe they did not do such a good job, but that is beside the point. Then, you have a Spiritual Family into which you are born in a new world, and you develop and you become educated so that you are a spiritual presence in life.

<a id="p19"></a>Now, when you first begin to realize this, it is very important to know that you will be like a child in this new realm. Like all little children, you will want everything but will actually have capacity for very little. Therefore, it takes time to understand how your wants are relative and dependent upon your capacity. Just like a little child wants to have everything interesting for themselves, they must learn what things are and what things are for good and what things are not for good. They must develop a capacity for having things and for being responsible.

<a id="p20"></a>The same is true with your spiritual heritage and destiny. At the beginning you want it all. "I want it all!" But you cannot really carry very much yet because your capacity is quite limited and your education has not been brought about. So, you must be raised within your Spiritual Family and educated as well so that you can assume the responsibilities.

<a id="p21"></a>Therefore, we wish to talk of achievement in terms of your Spiritual Family and your heritage, which you are privileged to bring into this world from beyond. We applaud all achievement for good in life. Yet our role is to cultivate this greater level of achievement. To prepare for this requires a great deal, yet whatever it requires is small compared to what it gives. It is an achievement that at the outset looks very intangible. You do not quite know what you are doing. It is not like you are going to build a better car or a business. That all seems very predictable. Many of the steps seem to be laid out in front of you. You merely need to follow them and be intelligent and committed to what you are doing.

<a id="p22"></a>In matters regarding your true spiritual heritage, it is much more mysterious. The steps are not so evident at the beginning. You enter because something inside of you is moving you to do that. To trust this requires that you relinquish self-hatred and self-doubt and begin to honor the Spiritual Presence that is emerging inside. You are taken through various stages of initiation. Then anything you do will have a lasting impact on the world-not only because of what you do physically, but because of the state of mind you are in. That, in essence, is the greatest achievement, but it requires the greatest preparation as well. We don't expect this of all people. In fact, we only aim at a few people, but everyone who is closely aligned with these people will be raised as well. So, our job is to initiate these individuals, but for everyone we have important ideas, too.

<a id="p23"></a>In order to do something important in life, your life must become simplified, directed and more concentrated. If I say to someone, "Go become a surgeon," well, to become a surgeon requires a great deal. It is not something you can do in your spare time. That is achievement.

<a id="p24"></a>Therefore, with all the talk of achievement and the expectations of others for achievement and the expectations of the human race for achievement, it is very important to see what achievement really is and what it requires. That is why I want to talk now about relationships.

<a id="p25"></a>There is something very important to see when you look at people who are involved in great achievements. Often they are not very well-rounded people. They are very advanced in one area and not so advanced in other areas. In terms of purely worldly achievement, this is always the case. In terms of achievement at a higher level, this cannot be the case. Purely worldly achievement requires using relationships to bring about the intended result, but achievement at a higher level has an entirely different emphasis on relationship. Here relationship is the result. What you achieve, you achieve in relationship. If you happen to build a building, or bridge or hospital, that is secondary. In fact, people who are achieving something in terms of this mysterious thing I am talking about, rarely do anything! They don't leave lasting things. They leave lasting relationships. Did Jesus Christ build anything? Did he carve his initials anywhere? Buddha? What did he do that is tangible? What is their achievement? They made it possible for many people to achieve. They ignited relationship at a very high level.

<a id="p26"></a>Of course, people do very stupid things too, but that must be expected. You cannot judge a teaching based upon what people do with it, and anyone who is giving the teaching must know this. They must know that it will be horribly disfigured by subsequent practitioners. This is a very important thing to think about.

<a id="p27"></a>So, a person who is involved in worldly achievement will use relationships to meet his or her aim. But for a higher level of achievement, relationships are your aim and those who achieve at the level of relationship will leave the greatest imprint, the most lasting effect. They will produce a motion that will stay in motion for a very long time.

<a id="p28"></a>All of this is very relevant to what you want to do with your life. Do you want to squander your life in little things which don't satisfy you, or do you want to concentrate on something very important? Many people seem to be happy with little things. That is okay. But these people usually don't come to hear me talk. People come to hear me talk because they have a deeper hunger that has manifested itself, at least to some degree. Many people come who are merely curious. "I want to go and see what this is" and they laugh about it later. That is okay. People laugh all the time about this. I don't mind. Then other people come because something is stirring in their life and they must do something about it. They must give it some recognition and some validation, and that is good.

<a id="p29"></a>If you want to achieve something purely tangible, then all you have to do is dedicate all your time and energy to doing that and use all of your talents and the talents of other people to help you do that, and you will make a strong impression in life. There are other factors involved in this, but generally that is it. Not that difficult! But to achieve a greater achievement in the realm of relationship, well, that is something else. You cannot have part of you being an idiot and another little part of you being brilliant and be a brilliant idiot who can do one thing very well and in everything else, you're handicapped.

<a id="p30"></a>Our emphasis, then, is on this other achievement. Any step in this direction makes you more powerful, more generous, wiser and more efficacious in life. Here you must be concentrated too, but it is much more mysterious. Your poor mind won't have much to hold onto because your spirit is moving things. That is good. It is a different kind of training, a different preparation for a different goal. Here you will achieve something that affects the Mystery of life, where all meaning comes from, whereas in tangible matters you are focusing on something physical that needs to happen. That is very commendable, and I don't want to belittle it. But these two levels of achievement are in different realms.

<a id="p31"></a>To see where you are aiming yourself, you must take stock of your own nature. If you thought your life was to achieve something and it hasn't happened so far, well, it's a good chance that is not your goal. People think, "Well, I would be so magnificent if only I had no barriers!" I cannot tell you what a ridiculous thing it is to think like that. It is not helpful. What that says is that barriers have all the power, and who you are is something meek, only a potentiality. It belittles what is real and gives great weight and authority to what is not real. This is not helpful! You will always end up condemning yourself and justifying error, so please don't think like that. There are always barriers. You will always have limitations in this life. You will have psychological limitations and physical limitations.

<a id="p32"></a>That ceases to be a problem when you are involved in something greater, however. That is why, in the most practical sense, true healing deals with being involved in something greater. When you have nothing greater to be involved in, you fall back on wants and fears. That is the prison house because what you want in that little realm is hardly enough to satisfy you. And so there is a constant war with the self to achieve little things which aren't really wanted anyway. True resolution comes because you are assuming something greater that pulls you out of this morass and allows you to concentrate, which you must do.

<a id="p33"></a>I have often given the example of the house on fire because if the house were on fire, everyone would jump into high gear. Who cares if you wanted a soul mate? House on fire! MY GOD! Who cares if you wanted more pay for your job? House on fire! Who cares if you wanted a body that never aches? House on fire! Everyone goes into gear. Do you wonder why people let situations become intolerable? That is a very marked tendency in human beings, both individually and collectively. You wait until the situation is dire because everyone must go into action then. But you cannot thrive upon catastrophe alone. Emergency is not enough to give you a sense of real purpose in life. Desperation is not a good foundation to live upon.

<a id="p34"></a>You all are remarkably free to consider what you really want to achieve in life. There have been times when Zuwinal and I have given perspective on the Greater Community of Worlds to illustrate your remarkable freedom and how much we encourage you to take advantage of it. Most of the Universe on the physical level is enslaved in contrast to your life, which is relatively free and casual. Often, teaching greater things works better with people who are enslaved because they take it more seriously, but it takes longer for them to move into a position where they can do something with it.

<a id="p35"></a>So, you are all very free. What do you want to do? You can have little things, but they are not very satisfying. You live in as much a spiritual and a mental world as a physical one. To be fully responsible means that you are accountable in all three realms. You do not neglect the physical because you are only interested in spiritual matters. Likewise, you do not neglect the mental because you are only interested in physical things. Achievement, at the level that I am pointing to, requires accountability in all three. Achievement on a purely physical level, a manifest level, requires accountability only in two, the physical and the mental, though in the mental it may not require a great deal. If you are clever, rational and patient, you can do a great deal because there is not much competition.

<a id="p36"></a>Sometimes, people think I am too hard on everyone, but I'm not. I am too easy on everyone. The reason I seem too hard is that I know who you are! So, I offer great things. If I did not think you were great, I would offer little things. If I thought people were sort of stupid, I would offer things for stupid people. But because people are greater, I offer things that are great. You must do something great to know you are great because this is a world where things are done. When things are done, they are demonstrated and that is the level of teaching here. Things are demonstrated. That is achievement!

<a id="p37"></a>So, what do we do as Teachers? We teach about relationship. Anytime people come together based upon a deeper impulse which we call Knowledge, something remarkable happens. But because this does not happen very often, it seems to be too rare to rely upon. You have created an environment here where people can have this kind of recognition, and that is good, yet it requires that you reevaluate relationships and what attracts people together. Here demonstration is very important.

<a id="p38"></a>People come together for many reasons. A great deal of that is for unlearning things. It is for disillusionment. It is not easy, and we wish to save people these pains, but we can only do so much. When you become tired of little things that are temporary and do not last, there is a good chance in a very sober moment you will say, "Oh, my God, this is my life! What am I doing with my life?" And you will perhaps stumble across something that has been waiting for you all along. That is very important.

<a id="p39"></a>Disappointment always opens a door, and it can be a remarkably rewarding experience if you can take advantage of it. Sometimes people only want temporary comforts in relationship. Sometimes people want an extended holiday. Sometimes people want something really lasting. It is very good to look and see what you want in this regard because relationships based upon Knowledge will be lasting. Their effects will be with you forever, even beyond this life. That is how permanent these relationships are. The discernment that you need to develop will give you a real sense of their contrast to everything else.

<a id="p40"></a>Your purpose in life is waiting to be discovered once you have reached a state of maturity. One of the qualities of maturity is that you begin to value things that last. You want to invest in things that have promise not only for the present but for the future as well. You look for things that are more substantial, and you look for different qualities in people. In relationships you look for compatibility now, not just beauty or excitement or romance or infatuation. You look for compatibility, honesty, productivity, and so forth. Different criteria. These things are not very valued in this society, unfortunately. Here in your society, romance is the ultimate expression of union-self abandonment in a profound love state where you forget all problems past, present and future to have a glorious moment with another person. For this moment, you will sacrifice a great deal and will expend your resources to a great extent.

<a id="p41"></a>In maturity you are not going for things that are momentary. You want things that last. You want to build something. You are thinking not only of immediate satisfaction. You are thinking of future providorship. You are thinking of yourself in terms of making a contribution, not only for the present time but in time to come. So, all of this becomes much bigger. You are living in a bigger world. You are seeing yourself participating in a greater time frame. This is maturity. Out of this comes a desire for achievement that is markedly different from your former state.

<a id="p42"></a>That is why we have been at times very hard on adolescent mentality, which is quite rampant here. It would be fun to have this mentality, if it were not so tragic. It is actually very silly, but it is unfortunate that so many resources are being spent for it while it provides so little.

<a id="p43"></a>As I have said, your worldly family initiates you into the world, but your Spiritual Family initiates you into the Mystery. You were born from the Mystery. You will die into the Mystery. Mystery is where you have come from. Everything that is lasting comes from Mystery. Everything that is transitory comes from the manifestation. There is Mystery in manifestation, but you cannot go too far into that Mystery or you will go beyond the manifestation and come back to the same great place.

<a id="p44"></a>There are two things that last in this world: There are tangible, physical manifestations and there is relationship. Of the two, relationship is far more lasting because it continues to be passed on long after your death. After all, buildings fall down and society changes, but what you have begun continues to move. When you begin to experience your Spiritual Family, you enter a whole new realm of relationship. There are many illusions that go along with spirituality, of course, and they are hopeful things. But when you begin education with your Spiritual Family-those individuals both within and beyond the world who are working on your spiritual development directly-your education will require that illusions be seen as such and be discerned from those things that are really true.

<a id="p45"></a>There is kind of a romance here, too, and that is all right because it is predictable. Without direct experience, what can you have but assumptions? So, the sooner you get to direct experience the better because assumptions become very cherished, particularly if they become the foundation of your identity. Then, even if they are not true, you will not want to admit it for fear that your life will be wasted. If an idea lasts too long and if an identity is built upon it, it will be very hard to relinquish. That is why ideas need to stay in motion and not become solid. Your true identity is beyond all ideas. Ideas can either help or hinder you in your progress. They are temporary expedients.

<a id="p46"></a>Now, this matter of relationship with each other is not really that complex. Everyone wants everything right away, but things that are important don't come immediately. They must come at the right time, and you must prepare for them. There are not many people who are prepared for successful marriage. That's why there is so much divorce. It's not hard to get married-not really-if you really want it. Just talk someone into loving you, find out what they want, try to be that, and for a period of time they will be convinced. Perhaps.

<a id="p47"></a>So, there is a great deal of experimentation in relationships. This is necessary to a point, but again we are aiming for something substantial and lasting. When you begin to realize your true powers of discernment, it will become increasingly difficult to make mistakes in relationships. Ultimately, it will become very hard to make any mistake. At the beginning you have this resource to fall back on. We call it Knowledge and we teach it.

<a id="p48"></a>For real achievement, the whole area of relationship must be re-examined. If you want something lasting, it requires different criteria. It is great to fall in love, but it often does not lead anywhere, and it carries a very great price. Likewise, it is fun to have a party and make love and all that, but it all carries a price. As you get a little older, you begin to realize the price-what it has cost you to invest yourself greatly in little things. So, if you feel you have reached a place where you want something truly substantive and lasting, it requires a different criteria for relationships. It requires patience, too.

<a id="p49"></a>There are only two things in life: There is relationship and there is ideas. As you abandon ideas, you become more available for relationship. So, don't look for new ideas, for you will have to abandon those too.

<a id="p50"></a>When I say relationship, people always think of romance because at this level that is what most relationship seems to be. Romance is a very small part of life. Romance is simply enjoyment between people who are united. When did it become the sole object of life where people will spend all their money and their time preparing themselves for this little romantic dance that they do, which at best has some glorious moments? Very intoxicating it is, but not much love comes out of it.

<a id="p51"></a>When I say relationship, people think, "Oh, boy meets girl. How wonderful!" I am not talking about that. I am talking in a bigger way, when you are really united with someone beyond personal interests. As you let yourself follow what is being given to you, it will make you ever more reliant and ever more sensitive to the Presence of your Spiritual Family. After all, to become truly united with someone, you must rely on them, yes? That is hard to achieve in a society that places such a premium on individual self-reliance, where you are all-powerful and don't need anyone. A true island! A fortress! This is not the way to undo pathetic needs. Great relationships are established because individuals can rely upon each other. This is not simply because they have seen each other and have felt a deep affinity. Affinity is not yet relationship. Emotional attraction is not yet relationship. It is only potentiality.

<a id="p52"></a>When people do something in life together, that establishes relationship. When they carry weight together and when they rely upon each other, that establishes relationship. That is why we are interested in people doing things together, not simply being mesmerized by their recognition of one another. Recognition is important, but it is not the substance and foundation of relationship. You will have great recognition with individuals that you will not be able to do anything with. Tremendous feeling of affinity, but in life you can do very little together. How often have people married based upon the experience of affinity, only to find out that in real life they could do little together? How often?

<a id="p53"></a>You cannot carry on substantial physical achievement and spiritual achievement simultaneously. One must follow another because they both require too much time. It is possible that someone who has achieved something physically that is very substantial will have a real emergence spiritually and vice-versa. You cannot succeed in two realms simultaneously, though many people are trying currently. They are trying very hard to be magnificent in all aspects and do you know what? They don't make much progress in any aspect. You see, you can't trick the way things are and find a new way to slip through the loophole in life. Look back in history. You are not smarter than people used to be! If you really want to find out the Mystery of your life, if that is the direction you choose, then that will draw all of your primary energy. If you wish to be involved in manifest life, then that will draw all of your energy. People who want it all don't go very far because you must make choices along the way. You let go of that to have this or this to have that. Why is this true? Because your Spiritual Family is extremely powerful and the attraction to your Spiritual Family is a stronger one than the world itself. Your attempt to maintain a precarious balance cannot last in the face of real forces. Likewise, the world is a real force and will captivate you completely, leaving little time or energy for other things.

<a id="p54"></a>Do not think your barriers can keep you from Knowledge. Your desire for it is greater than the barriers! If desire is weak, barriers are great. If desire is strong, barriers are little. Therefore, we don't look at barriers with a magnifying glass. We rekindle the desire for truth and Knowledge because these things give meaning everywhere and provide all good things in this world.
