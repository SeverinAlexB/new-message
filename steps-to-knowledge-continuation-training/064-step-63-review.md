# Step 63: Review

<a id="p1"></a>Reread carefully the previous six steps and consider what they are asking of you, what they are giving you and how you may further use them in the future. Then turn to your Four Pillars
Review, once again checking, checking and checking again to see if there is anything that you need to know, if there is anything that is amiss that requires your attention. Look and see if there is anything that is producing consistent discomfort--any relationship or any activity that constantly reminds you that there is a problem or a conflict. Pay special attention should you find this during your Review.

<a id="p2"></a>One of the difficulties that all students of Knowledge face is that there are not many people who have undertaken such a preparation and so there are not many glaring examples of success. Here you must be like pioneers that are breaking new ground and blazing a new trail. You are not the only ones to follow the path of initiation most certainly, but the numbers of those who have advanced are not really that great. And you are following a new way. You are creating the pathway, so you must become strong and have great self-confidence. to make mistakes. They are important Be willing to experiment. Be willing to extend beyond your normal limits. It is appropriate here.
