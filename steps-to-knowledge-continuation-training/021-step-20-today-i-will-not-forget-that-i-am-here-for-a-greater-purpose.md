# Step 20: Today I Will Not Forget That I Am Here for a Greater Purpose.

<a id="p1"></a>You are indeed here for a greater purpose. But do not define that purpose for yourself because at the appointed time, it will become known to you, and it will not simply be the result of personal preference or ambition. It will be something that will be felt and deeply known. And it will be real, and you will know it to be true. Until that point of recognition, you are building the foundation for that recognition. When you build a house, you do not build the upper floors first.
You start with the foundation and you build up. You do not begin by laying the wallpaper and putting in the furnishings, for there is nothing to contain them. You begin by building the foundation and the primary structure of the building. And you do this in a step by step manner.
And you follow a plan. And you develop skills along the way. And you exercise skills you already have. And you learn new ones as well.

<a id="p2"></a>Therefore, today practice this building exercise in your long practice in stillness and in your hourly practices in awareness and discernment. Build this inner awareness, this position of observing without condemnation but looking to know. You need to know. You want to know.
