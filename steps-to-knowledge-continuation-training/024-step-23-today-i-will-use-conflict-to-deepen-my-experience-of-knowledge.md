# Step 23: Today I Will Use Conflict to Deepen my Experience of Knowledge.

<a id="p1"></a>Use conflict today to remind yourself that there is only Knowledge and the need for Knowledge.
Using this approach gives you a way to see beyond the complexities that confuse people at the outset and to see into the deeper nature of the human predicament and your own predicament.

<a id="p2"></a>Because the world is emerging into the Greater Community, you need these greater capabilities.
This is not merely an option for those who are gifted and wise. It is really essential for the continuance of humanity and for the freedom of humanity. These become, then, essential rather than adjunct. They become primary to life because they are primary to life. And you will be able to see that they are at the forefront of your own personal priorities rather than something that you easily forget, that you have to be reminded of as you go along.

<a id="p3"></a>You are sent to serve the world. To do this, you must recognize conflict But you must approach it in a certain way or you cannot bring resolution there. You cannot be a force for resolution, for conflict will draw you in and will overwhelm you, and you will not be able to understand it You will give your power away to it. You will give your awareness away to it It will overwhelm you and you will be lost to it. See this around you. The evidence is everywhere. See how even today you are captivated by things that upset or disturb you to the point where you cannot see and you cannot know and you cannot retrieve yourself to a position of discernment.

<a id="p4"></a>Even in your practice in stillness today you can see this. How to clear the mind and to quiet the mind can sometimes seem to be a great effort because the captivation has already occurred.
Somehow you got swept downstream. and now you have to go downstream and find yourself and pull yourself out of the water because you got washed away! And though the current may be strong, you must pull yourself ashore.

<a id="p5"></a>Practice this in your hourly practices. Consider this today.
