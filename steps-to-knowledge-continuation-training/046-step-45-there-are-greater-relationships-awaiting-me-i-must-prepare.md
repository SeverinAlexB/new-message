# Step 45: There Are Greater Relationships Awaiting Me. I Must Prepare.

<a id="p1"></a>There are greater relationships awaiting you. And you must prepare. Part of the preparation is freeing yourself from those engagements that may be inappropriate for you now, even if they were appropriate for you at an earlier time. How can you find these great relationships if you are not free to participate? You will be ambivalent regarding them if you think that their arrival in your life will upset all of your other commitments to people. Even the arrival of the Teaching in
Greater Community Spirituality will arouse considerable anxiety if you think it will upset or disrupt the delicate balance that you have in your life at this moment. For the Creator’s first purpose is to unburden you. This requires change and readjustment. If this change is not welcome, if the need for it is not understood and supported, then the answer to your great need must wait and your prayers must be left unanswered until a later time.

<a id="p2"></a>There are greater relationships awaiting you. You must be available to them. Do not fill up your emptiness or your loneliness with people. Do not become socially engaged unless it serves a greater and immediate purpose. Let Knowledge be your guide. Follow your natural inclinations.
Resist your social conditioning if it obstructs this. This is choosing within yourself. It is a shift in your internal allegiance. It is saying "No" to your own bondage and "Yes" to your freedom.
Knowledge will commit you totally to the right people, but it will remain silent with everyone else.

<a id="p3"></a>Think of this throughout the day, and when you practice stillness, be close to Knowledge.
Dedicate yourself at the beginning of this practice. And upon the hour, remember to look and to listen to see where you are, how you feel and what is going on around you.
