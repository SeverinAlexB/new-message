# Step 16: There Are Greater Forces at Work in the World.

<a id="p1"></a>There are indeed greater forces at work in the world, and you can feel their movement, and you can bear witness to their reality. Here you look beyond yourself into the world. If you can look without prejudice and without fear and denial, you will be able to see very far, farther even than you thought you could. The fact is that most people do not look and therefore do not see. And if they do look, they look with so many conditions and prerequisites and requirements that they cannot really grasp what they are seeing.

<a id="p2"></a>If you are not dominated by your own mind, then you are free to look and to see. If you are not dominated by your past, then you are able to see things as they are today, in a new way.

<a id="p3"></a>Today, then, is an opportunity to see where you are going and recognize the greater forces in the world. This puts you into a larger arena of life. You become a witness to greater things. Perhaps at the beginning you could only see a tiny, tiny bit. But as we practice in your ongoing preparation, you will be able to see more as you proceed, for you will develop the eyes and the approach to see and the willingness to see and the desire to see.

<a id="p4"></a>Beyond your practice in stillness today and your hourly practices in awareness and discernment, spend time just being still looking at the world, perhaps a moment here or a moment there, considering the greater forces that are at work in the world. It is important here not to have any conclusions but simply to ask the question, "What are the greater forces that are working in the world?" Just keep asking the question, without supplying the answer. It is your experience here that will be the real answer. To have this experience, you must ask the question and you must begin to look.
