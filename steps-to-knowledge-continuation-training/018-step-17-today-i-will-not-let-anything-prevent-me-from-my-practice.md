# Step 17: Today I Will Not Let Anything Prevent me from my Practice.

<a id="p1"></a>The world is so dominating that people acquiesce to it habitually. It takes real determination to maintain a greater focus in life, particularly if you are preoccupied with difficulties and challenges along the way. But today we practice strengthening this determination so that you may have the gift of looking and seeing.

<a id="p2"></a>Upon the hour practice looking and seeing—-checking the mental environment, checking how you feel, checking what is going on inside of you, taking this moment to come back to yourself.

<a id="p3"></a>In your practice in stillness, the mind yields to Knowledge (if?) you are able to experience something greater than the mind. Throughout the day as well, try to maintain this awareness and see in the observations of others how captivating the world is and how captivating the mind is.
Ultimately, the only thing that can keep you from Knowledge is the mind, but if the mind has given itself to the world, then it seems like the world is dominating people, but in reality it is the mind.

<a id="p4"></a>Observe this. You will not have to look far. It is all around you. You can look at your own experience to see how close at hand this is and how important it is for you to remain present where you are with what you are doing. Today’s practice will strengthen that awareness and determination.
