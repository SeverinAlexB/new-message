# Overview

<a id="p1"></a>The Steps Continuation Training continues the life-changing journey begun in Steps to Knowledge. Containing 84 Steps, or practices, The Continuation Training takes you further toward the realization of your purpose and destiny in life.

<a id="p2"></a>If you have completed the journey in Steps to Knowledge twice and have followed the instructions without altering the curriculum, then you are ready to begin this more advanced study.

<a id="p3"></a>The Steps Continuation Training is available to students of the New Message at the [New Knowledge Library](https://newknowledgelibrary.org/) store.

<a id="p4"></a>The Steps Continuation Training is the second book of Volume 3 of the New Message.
