# Step 39: Let me Understand my Relationships Today.

<a id="p1"></a>Today think of every person with whom you are directly engaged—-all of your primary relationships--and ask yourself these questions:

<a id="p2"></a>• Do I have purpose with this person?
• Is our relationship being expressed appropriately?
• Are we sharing the same destiny?

<a id="p3"></a>Ask these three questions of everyone with whom you are directly engaged and listen carefully for the answer. You will be tempted to answer this question yourself, but do not trust these answers. Instead, wait for something deeper to come. The answer may not come in the moment.
It may come far later. But the fact that you are asking the question and listening for the answer is the important thing here.

<a id="p4"></a>Do this practice intermittently throughout the day, and in your long practice in stillness listen carefully, without asking any questions. Feel the presence of Knowledge. Open yourself to
Knowledge. Let Knowledge be with you and speak with you. And as you practice upon the hour, listen carefully. Look carefully. Pay attention to where you are and what is happening within yourself. Life will reveal its secrets to you if you can do this. And today is your opportunity to practice doing so.
