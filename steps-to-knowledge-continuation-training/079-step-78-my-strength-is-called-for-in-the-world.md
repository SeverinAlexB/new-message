# Step 78: My Strength is Called for in the World.

<a id="p1"></a>Your strength is called for in the world, most assuredly. But do you have the strength? Do you have the wisdom? Are you ready? Can you respond? Can you deliver what you came here to deliver? Can you recognize those people who are essential for you to know and to associate with? And can you look past those who are not essential for you to know even though you may feel great affection for them?

<a id="p2"></a>A relationship is only as good as the strength that its participants bring to it. It is the sum of your accomplishments. Your relationship with others, your primary relationships, reflect this. Your relationship with the world reflects this. You can have a dysfunctional relationship with the
Creator, and in fact this is quite common. What is the strength you bring to this relationship?
What kind of discernment do you bring here? The world is calling for your strength. This is a relationship with the world. What is your responsibility here?

<a id="p3"></a>People say they have a calling and they want a calling in life, and perhaps they will really dream of it and really desire it, but are they ready? Only the Wise will know. That is why the real
Spiritual Teachers, the Unseen Ones, and even those who are still in physical life, do not show up everywhere giving everything to everybody. Perhaps they are not the popular teachers that are out there disseminating the "Ancient Texts" or the "Secret Teachings" to the general public. The more advanced the teacher, the more discernment they must have, the more care they must use regarding students and the more discrete they must be about what they know and what they can do.

<a id="p4"></a>So the world is calling you, but are you ready? Life is ready. The world is ready. Are you ready?
Do you have strong enough relationships? Do you have the self-confidence? Ask yourself these questions today.

<a id="p5"></a>In your practice in stillness, simply be still and listen so that you may become stronger. Exercise this strength upon the hour when you are out in the world to see where you are and what is happening. Stay in contact with yourself so that you may become strong in every situation that you enter.
