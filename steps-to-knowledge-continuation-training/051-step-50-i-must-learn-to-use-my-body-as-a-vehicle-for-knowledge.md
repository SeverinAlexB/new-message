# Step 50: I Must Learn to Use my Body as a Vehicle for Knowledge.

<a id="p1"></a>If the mind serves the Spirit and the body serves the mind, then everything serves Spirit. Your physical body then becomes your key link with the world. Its promise here is to become an instrument of communication and contribution. For this to happen, the body requires a developed degree of health and ability. If you do not have a healthy relationship with your body, then it cannot fulfill this greater function. That is why it is important for all students of Knowledge to maintain an exercise program and to eat very well in order to elevate their health and physical ability. You do not want your body failing you after you have done so much work preparing the way for Knowledge.

<a id="p2"></a>We are saddened that people do not value their physical being after so much work has been done to bring them into the world. But we understand this neglect because if you do not know your purpose or your greater reality, then you will think of your body as a prison and you will exercise your self-abuse by misusing your body. It will then become an image of your self-neglect or selfabuse. We seek to correct this destructive attitude by reminding you that the body is an instrument for communication and contribution. It must be maintained with a high degree of health and vitality for this purpose.

<a id="p3"></a>Think today of how you may elevate your level of physical health. Do not think spiritual development is only working at the level of your mind or emotions because your body needs certain things, and these are already apparent to you. If your body is to serve you, then you must take very good care of it.

<a id="p4"></a>Today practice stillness and upon the hour exercise awareness and discernment. Throughout the day think of your body as a greater instrument and recognize what you can do to support it and maintain it in this way.
