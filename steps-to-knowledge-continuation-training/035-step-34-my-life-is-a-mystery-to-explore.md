# Step 34: My Life is a Mystery to Explore.

<a id="p1"></a>We continue this practice from STEPS TO KNOWLEDGE because it is so very true. Your life is a great mystery. It is not simply the accumulation of your activities each day—-your work, your hobbies, your family obligations, your tendencies, your fears and your phobias. This is only the outer part of yourself, the part that is shaped and conditioned by your worldly experiences. There is a greater mystery for you to explore and to discover.

<a id="p2"></a>Enter the realm of stillness in your long practice today and utilize this great mystery in exercising the strength of awareness and discernment upon the hour. Bring this mystery into your experience, into your daily life, into your mundane circumstances, and with it you shall bring strength, grace and resolution, both for yourself and for others.
