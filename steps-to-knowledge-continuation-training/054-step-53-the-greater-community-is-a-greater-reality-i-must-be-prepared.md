# Step 53: The Greater Community is a Greater Reality. I Must Be Prepared.

<a id="p1"></a>The Greater Community is a greater reality. And it is overshadowing everything that you are doing today and everything that you will do tomorrow. To comprehend the Greater Community and to prepare for it, you must have a very firm foundation. Your life cannot be collapsing underneath you. You must build a table with four strong legs and be able to stand upon it.
Otherwise, you will not be able to see and you will not be able to give. You will be one of life’s casualties, not one of life’s contributors.

<a id="p2"></a>The Greater Community and the preparation for the Greater Community represent a tremendous threshold. You do not get to choose this, for the Greater Community is upon you. The choice is whether to respond or not to respond. The choice is whether to prepare or not to prepare.
Therefore, in learning STEPS TO KNOWLEDGE, you learn to build the foundation, for that is half the preparation right there. If you do not build the foundation, well, you cannot really exercise your greater abilities. You will not become a force in the world, a force for good.

<a id="p3"></a>So, you work simultaneously at all levels—-relationships, work, health, spiritual development,
Greater Community awareness—-because that is half the preparation. The other half of the preparation is in learning about the Mental Environment. This represents a more elevated level of training and preparation. For this, you must have a tremendous foundation. Your life must be in order, and you must be right with your own life.

<a id="p4"></a>Today think about these things. Look carefully. See how today’s lesson can be for you and for others. Practice stillness in your deep meditation. And exercise your authority by gaining awareness and discernment in the moment upon the hour when you are out in the world.
Remember here that you are preparing for something greater, something that is already happening, something that needs your participation and awareness.
