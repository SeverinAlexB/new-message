# Step 24: The Unseen Ones Are with Me.

<a id="p1"></a>The Unseen Ones are with you. They are watching you. And they are assisting you by strengthening Knowledge and your awareness and desire for Knowledge. They need not speak to you today, for that is hot your work at this time. But their Presence can give reassurance and an important reminder that your presence in the world is for a greater purpose, not merely for survival and gratification. They then serve as a reminder of your Ancient Home, of your Spiritual
Family and of your greater purpose in being here.

<a id="p2"></a>Today, consider that they are with you and watching over you as they watch over others as well.
And be grateful that you are in their care, for to understand that your responsibilities here are great is that they cannot do your work for you. Therefore, today we once again practice stillness in one long practice period. And we practice discernment and awareness upon the hour because we are doing the work in the world. YOU are doing the work in the world. This is your contribution to the Unseen Ones. And this is your gift to everyone.
