# Step 33: I Practice for Myself and for All Others Who Seek to Be Free.

<a id="p1"></a>Think of yourself as practicing for all who seek to be free. See yourself as fighting for their freedom as well as your own. See yourself as a forerunner, as a leader of others in this regard, for you are blazing a new trail. You are beginning a new tradition. You are bringing the freshness of
Spirit into the religions of the world and into the reality of the world. For The Way of
Knowledge is The Way of Spirit as it truly expresses itself in your world and throughout the
Greater Community.

<a id="p2"></a>Therefore, think of yourself as working for others as well as for yourself. This will strengthen your resolve and strengthen your commitment. You will then be able to use each day to bring greater strength, purpose and hope into the world.

<a id="p3"></a>In your longer practice in stillness and in your hourly practices in awareness and in discernment, exercise this strength and this authority, which are given to you to use this day.
