# Step 80: I Have a Gift That I Share with Others.

<a id="p1"></a>Your great gift is meant to be given in conjunction with the giving of others. You don't get to be a superstar in reality. Your contribution comes through relationship. You have part of the gift.
They have part of the gift. You give together. It is your relationship that gives and makes the giving possible. This serves as a perfect antidote to all personal ambition and self-glorification.
Even the advanced man or woman of Knowledge represents relationship. Without this relationship--with the Unseen Ones, with Knowledge, with others--their gift would only be a potential. Yet they have built the relationships. That is why they have advanced.

<a id="p2"></a>You are building the relationships. That is why you have the promise of advancement. Your giving is shared with others. This is the key. Find the others that have the other part of your gift and become mature with them. Bring into these relationships all that you have learned and allow others to teach you more. This will generate real humility. Real humility is the recognition that you understand the nature of power and its expression in the universe.

<a id="p3"></a>Exercise this today in your practice in stillness and in your hourly practice. Exercise the awareness that a Greater Power is working in your life, that you are working in your life and that everything is working together. It is towards this that you are working today and every day as you practice as a student of Knowledge. You have not achieved this balance yet, but it is growing. Each step in that direction gives you strength, power and confirmation, which are the elements that redeem you to yourself and to the world.
