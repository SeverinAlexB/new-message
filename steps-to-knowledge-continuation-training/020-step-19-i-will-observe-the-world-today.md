# Step 19: I Will Observe the World Today.

<a id="p1"></a>Continue the practice in observation. Observe the world today. You have a great opportunity to do this, regardless of where you are, for you are in the world and will be in the world all day today. Observe the world without judgment. You will see things you like and you will see things you don't like. You will see things that evoke anger or judgment, denial, happiness, preference, but today practice being more detached. Just observe things. And inside, as you practice, develop stillness.

<a id="p2"></a>To help you today, you will have one long practice in stillness. Bring yourself completely to your practice. It is a form of personal dedication. In most cases, you will be able to dispel anything that distracts you with this sense of dedication. In those situations where you cannot dispel the preoccupations of the mind, you simply become a detached witness to them. In this way, you bring yourself back to a place where you are close to Knowledge. For Knowledge watches all things and awaits the moment when true action must be undertaken.

<a id="p3"></a>Today we practice doing what Knowledge does. We practice doing what the Creator does. We practice being what we really are and being in harmony with what is true in life.
