# Step 6: My Mind is Here to Serve Knowledge.

<a id="p1"></a>Today's lesson reinforces much of what you have learned already, and this reinforcement is necessary because the mind without a true ruler will try to rule itself, and it makes a very poor guide in this respect. Indeed, it becomes more of a tyrant, and it is abusive, arrogant and selfdeprecating all at the same time. True harmony within yourself means that your mind is serving your Spirit and your body is serving your mind. Here the true value of the mind and the body are recognized and all self-abuse is forgotten. And abusive relationships are left behind.

<a id="p2"></a>In learning The Greater Community Way of Knowledge, the mind is highly valued. It is not cast away. It is not belittled. It is given instead its true place in the greater hierarchy within you. If you can enter into this understanding, you will have a true appreciation of the presence and the grace of the Creator in your life. For the answer is so clear and so close at hand. That which seemed inexplicable and so difficult to obtain is ever present with you, waiting for you to experience it.

<a id="p3"></a>Therefore, today value your mind, but recognize that it must serve a Greater Power-not a power in the world, not a power that assigns itself to you, not a power that you give yourself to without true consideration, but an inner power. And Knowledge will direct you to those people that you are to serve and who can serve you. Here, if Knowledge is your guide, then your relationship will be established on a true foundation and will have true promise as a result.

<a id="p4"></a>Today in your practice in stillness, allow your mind to serve Knowledge by yielding to
Knowledge, by becoming still like Knowledge is still. Let the mind come to its temple and bow down and say, "Today I give myself to you, the Great Spirit that lives within me and beyond me and throughout the universe." This is a yielding, and this yielding is an act of surrender and grace.
Upon the hour remember this as you learn to apply Knowledge in the world. Remember that your mind is here to serve Knowledge and Knowledge can only serve the world.
