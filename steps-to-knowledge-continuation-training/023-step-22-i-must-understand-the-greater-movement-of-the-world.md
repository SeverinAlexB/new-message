# Step 22: I Must Understand the Greater Movement of the World.

<a id="p1"></a>Here we continue to build your understanding of the greater movement of the world, but the desire for this must come from within you. And the capacity must be established or you cannot see and you cannot know. Understanding the world in this way gives a greater context for your being here, for greater purpose cannot be realized within a narrow context. You must see yourself in a bigger way. You must establish your relationship with the world on a true foundation. This is part of your foundation building, and it is part of the expansion of your understanding and the range and scope of your mental and emotional capabilities. If you think about it, you will see that there are so many things that people cannot respond to emotionally or intellectually. They just cannot entertain it. It is too big or too challenging or too different, and so it is just rejected out of hand.

<a id="p2"></a>But you are learning to be with life as it is and as it will be and to prepare for the future in a way that will enable you to be in the future with a greater strength and efficacy.

<a id="p3"></a>Therefore, we turn our eyes to the world again and we look while we ask the question, "What do
I need to know about the world’s evolution and destiny?" We ask this question without giving answers, without emphasizing our preferences, without expressing our fears. These things may come to mind, and you will recognize them. But we are waiting for a deeper understanding. We are calling for it. We are evoking it. We are asking the Creator to reveal it to us. We are calling upon Knowledge. We are opening ourselves to Knowledge.

<a id="p4"></a>Practice today in addition to your one long practice in stillness and your hourly practices in awareness and discernment. Look and ask that you may see.
