# Step 70: Review

<a id="p1"></a>Review the past six steps and consider the message they are giving you. Let this message grow.
We are providing essential material here for your advancement and for your initiation into
Knowledge. But these things must be taken to heart and considered deeply over time. The quick and the easy way is for people who cannot make the journey. They buy postcards. They don't make their destinations. You are heading for a real destination now, and therefore you must consider things in a greater way. You must become deeper, more serious and more focused in your intent. This will give you a lightheartedness over time because your energy will be freed for greater things, and you will not feel burdened by so much that entraps people around you.

<a id="p2"></a>Return to your Four Pillars today and see if anything requires your attention. Be very conscientious here and very thorough. Look carefully. Ask if there is anything you need to know in each of the Four Pillars.
