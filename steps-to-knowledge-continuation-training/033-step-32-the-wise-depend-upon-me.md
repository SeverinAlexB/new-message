# Step 32: The Wise Depend Upon Me.

<a id="p1"></a>It is true. The Wise do depend upon you and many others like you, for you have come into the world to serve the world. Because of this, you serve a greater purpose and a greater hierarchy in the universe. This is your Spiritual Family. This is the great group that has brought you into the world to contribute to a world in need, a world whose emergence into the Greater Community has now arrived, whose needs are tremendous and whose predicament is great.

<a id="p2"></a>Therefore, do not look at yourself in a self-deprecating way. Do not think of yourself the way you have thought of yourself in the past, as weak and pathetic. Instead, reclaim your true authority and identity, even if you are only beginning to experience its reality, for in this way, you open yourself to receive the Great Truth that lives within you and that wishes to express itself through you. This is how God will redeem you. Reclaiming your strength and your true identity, learning the wisdom of the world and receiving the gift of Heaven—-these are the things that will call forth your strength and will cast out all the demons that live within your mind and around you, that plague you, haunt you and keep you living in the darkness of your own imagination.

<a id="p3"></a>Come forth today then in your own practice in stillness. Dedicate your practice as an act of submission and of strength—-submitting your personal preoccupations to a Greater Power and exercising the strength of that Greater Power within yourself.

<a id="p4"></a>In your hourly practices, exercise this strength and authority. Do not hesitate. You do this for yourself and for all others who await escape from the bondage of their conditioning and their own personal enslavement. This, then, is the great promise that you have come to fulfill, and great gifts can be given through you if you can fulfill and take advantage of this opportunity.
