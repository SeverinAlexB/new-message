# Step 44: If I Understand my Relationships, I Will Understand my Priorities.

<a id="p1"></a>Again we emphasize this matter of relationships and priorities, inner choosing, inner freedom, and the emergence of a greater purpose in your life. Certainly, the emergence of that greater purpose will change your obligations and your responsibilities. Surely it will create a fundamental shift here. Your position with everyone will change. With some people, that change will seem minor. With others, it will be quite significant.

<a id="p2"></a>Understanding, then, your relationships as a reflection of your values and how you view yourself becomes an important tool in understanding your own development at this time. If you feel overridden by the desires and the will of others, if you feel that you must carry forth social commitments regardless of your own sense of restraint or anxiety, clearly there is a problem here at hand. Clearly, your authority is not being exercised and your freedom is not being used.
Knowledge knows where you belong. Knowledge knows whom you must give yourself to.
Knowledge understands your real commitments in life and where they must be placed. Any commitments outside of this are essentially weak and unsteady and very subject to question. You must question them.

<a id="p3"></a>Beyond your practice in stillness and your hourly remembrances of awareness and discernment, again consider your obligations to others. Ask yourself, "Is this relationship appropriate for me?"
And if you feel that it is, ask yourself, "To what degree must I commit myself here?" Be careful not to answer your own questions, however, for the real answer is waiting for you.
