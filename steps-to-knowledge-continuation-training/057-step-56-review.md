# Step 56: Review

<a id="p1"></a>As you have done before, review the past six lessons. Then begin your Four Pillars exercise. This past week you have been doing a lot of looking, considering things deeply. This is good. This is the right use of the mind. The mind seeks to be employed. If it becomes self-employed, it becomes an erratic and destructive force. The mind seeks a master. That is one of the reasons why people give themselves to other authority figures because they do not know of Knowledge, who is the real master. Your mind as a servant is magnificent. Your mind without anything to serve is pathetic.

<a id="p2"></a>Think of these things today in your Review and be glad that you can exercise your mind in a truly productive and positive way. Do not worry about the results. They will come because you are building the strength to bring them forth and to accept them. You are building the foundation and with it the real power that you need.
