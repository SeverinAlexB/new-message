# Step 35: Review

<a id="p1"></a>In our Review today, we return to review your Four Pillars of Life—-the Pillar of Relationships, the Pillar of Career & Providership, the Pillar of Health and the Pillar of your Spiritual
Advancement. Utilize the same practice that you have been using through all of these review exercises. Go to each Pillar and ask, "Is there something I need to know regarding that Pillar?"
And if something comes to mind, ask again, "Is there something I need to know about this?" If something is there calling for you, go face it. Indeed, those things that need your attention are calling for you, and they will create a discomfort for you if they go unattended. You will feel out of integrity with yourself if they are neglected.

<a id="p2"></a>Therefore, utilize this Review to stay current with Knowledge within yourself. Carry forth the foundation building that is your spiritual purpose at this time, for with this foundation, you will become a foundation for others, and it is this foundation that is needed in the world at this time.
Carry forth your hourly practices and review the last six steps to see how they are affecting your life and how you may understand their messages ever more deeply.
