# Step 77: Review

<a id="p1"></a>Again review the past six steps, recalling your experience of them each day that you practiced them. Consider them anew and see what they are offering you, what they are telling you and how much they are freeing you from all of the forces in the world that would belittle you and hurt you.

<a id="p2"></a>Then return to the Four Pillars to find out if there is anything that you need to know. Out of these
Pillar Reviews you will see that there are certain activities that must be carried out to strengthen a certain Pillar at a certain time. Perhaps at this time you have insufficient funds and you need to earn more money. Perhaps you need a better job. Just make sure that this is essential. Make sure that the change is really needed. Ask yourself and listen. Ask others to vericate this for you, those whom you trust. Take your decision to them and say, "I think I need to do this. What do you think?" Give them the opportunity to respond. If their verication is good, they will go beyond their personal opinions and resonate from a place of Knowledge. You would be fortunate if you had such a friend or an ally.

<a id="p3"></a>Over the course of studying STEPS TO KNOWLEDGE, you will see that certain of your Pillars require work, and you must endeavor to work on them. When you come back for your next
Review, you will see what accomplishments you have made and what yet needs to be done. This then becomes a matter of maintaining your Pillars. New things need to be accomplished, and perhaps things are falling apart in a certain area. Perhaps certain things need your attention. This will give you an opportunity to recognize that.

<a id="p4"></a>You must be very strong in The Way of Knowledge. You must have a very solid foundation if you are to approach life and to approach the reality of the Greater Community.
