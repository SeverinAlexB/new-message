# Step 13: I Give Myself to my Practice Today.

<a id="p1"></a>No matter what is happening in your life at this moment, no matter what challenge you may be facing, or what difficulties may be preoccupying you, you have an opportunity today to apply everything to your practice and to deepen your practice. Here you participate in the world like your Creator, who uses everything for a greater purpose. The Creator does not come in and destroy the wicked and take control of everyone and everything and force a unity that people would resist and could not choose for themselves. Instead, everything is set in motion for redemption, for reunion, for contribution, for empowerment, for forgiveness, for true recognition.

<a id="p2"></a>Today your life gives you the tools to do this. It gives you the laboratory experiment. Use today.
In your practice time and in your hourly practices, use this. Stay with this. Everything the world teaches you about Knowledge and the denial of Knowledge or the ignorance of Knowledge. Use this. Let the world serve you through its demonstration. Observe yourself and see how easily your concentration is lost, how easily you give yourself away to things that seem to overtake you, things that have not nearly the power that you do. See how your fear overtakes you, overwhelms you, and that you become at its mercy. Today then can serve you in your practice if you are present.
