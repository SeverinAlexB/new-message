# Step 67: My Freedom is Awaiting Me.

<a id="p1"></a>What is freedom except the ability to unite with who you are and why you are here? The inability to do this represents the lack of freedom in all of its manifestations. Your freedom is awaiting you. Others’ freedom is awaiting them. And yet you suffer every day without it, and so do they.

<a id="p2"></a>Do not think that freedom will always be there for you and that it does not matter when you achieve it, for you are paying a great price and carrying a great burden without it. Feel this price;
Sense this burden. And you will want to advance. You will want to progress. You will not be satisfied with the compromises that you make with yourself or with the compromises that people make with themselves or with you. For this only prolongs suffering and misery and only keeps you from gaining access to your true gift and your true value in life. What benefit is there in doing this? What does this serve?

<a id="p3"></a>Often when people ask for things for themselves, they are only asking for things to prolong their misery. They are asking for the substitutes for happiness—-a new relationship, a new possession or a new experience or sensation captivates them, and they want it. And they pay so much for it.
But what are they really asking for here? What is the gift to them and to others that is truly lasting? Simple pleasures are fine and are a natural part of enjoyment in life, but dedicating yourself to them represents a serious problem and demonstrates that the person involved does not value his or her freedom or existence in the world.

<a id="p4"></a>Freedom is awaiting you. Come in your time of stillness practice to receive it and exercise it during your hourly practices where you come into the present to see where you are and how you feel. Freedom here is a great emphasis, not only for your personal liberation but because the world is emerging into a Greater Community of intelligent life. Power and freedom become very essential within this larger context. For power and freedom must be protected, developed and advanced here if they are to be safe guarded. Humanity is in a very vulnerable position in the
Greater Community. You will learn more about this as you proceed in your study of The Way of
Knowledge.

<a id="p5"></a>Therefore, think of your freedom not only for yourself and for those whom you can think of, but for the entire world. The world desperately needs men and women who can demonstrate this and speak for this, for this is one of the great needs of the world today.
