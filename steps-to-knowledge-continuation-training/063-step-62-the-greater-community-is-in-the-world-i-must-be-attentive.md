# Step 62: The Greater Community is in the World. I Must Be Attentive.

<a id="p1"></a>Beyond the normal range of your personal concerns and interests is the presence of the Greater
Community in the world. Whether you have had a direct encounter or not, you can discern this presence over time. It is a very distinct change in the Mental Environment and can affect the physical environment as well.

<a id="p2"></a>Be attentive today. Listen beyond the range of your sight and your hearing. Ask yourself, "How can I discern the presence of Greater Community forces in the world?" Ask this question at many different intervals today and listen. You might hear a word. You might get a sign. Something may be shown to you. The answer can come in many different ways. The important thing is you are asking the question and listening. If you receive something, ask yourself, "Is this true?" Be patient in finding the true answer. Most people are not patient enough to do this, and that is why the answers are so wrong.

<a id="p3"></a>Practice stillness in your meditation and practice awareness and discernment upon the hour.
Utilize your mind and your body as receptive instruments today, as instruments of discernment, for this is part of their function.
