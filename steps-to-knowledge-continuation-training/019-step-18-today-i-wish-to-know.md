# Step 18: Today I Wish to Know.

<a id="p1"></a>It is your desire for Knowledge and your capacity for Knowledge that will make it available to you. Today’s lesson is not a demand. It is an affirmation of your intention. You can demand
Knowledge to come to you, but it will not come, for it knows whether you are ready or not, and if there is no place for it to enter, it cannot enter. Here there must be no deception in your approach. There must be no manipulation in order to gain a personal advantage. Instead, you affirm your desire for Knowledge.

<a id="p2"></a>Affirm your desire for Knowledge in your long practice in stillness today by being completely present and not allowing this to be a fantasy session of any kind. Affirm your desire for
Knowledge by remembering upon the hour to stop for a moment, to be with your environment and to be with yourself. Affirm your desire for Knowledge by considering today how important this desire is and how lacking it seems around you. For you will see that even presented with simple problems in the world, people will avoid or deny them. They will go away from them, as if every problem were some kind of stinging pain for them or reminded them of some inner disabilities that the cannot tolerate.

<a id="p3"></a>You who are learning to face the world and to face yourself need today’s affirmation to give you the strength, the courage and the reassurance that you need.
