# Step 61: Today I Will Be Discrete.

<a id="p1"></a>Today be discrete. Carry on whatever conversation is necessary in your work, but do not reveal what is happening within you. Be aware of it, but keep it to yourself for today. If you feel prompted by another to talk about your experience, politely decline. Say, "I can't talk about that right now. I'm not ready to speak about that." Hold it within yourself as a conscious act.

<a id="p2"></a>Just as Knowledge is discrete with you, you must learn to be discrete with others. Think how discrete the Creator is! My goodness! There is a lot to be communicated. But it must be saved for the right person, the right moment and the right time. Who has such patience? The timing is crucial. Learn this and your words will have great power and you will not waste your life force.
You will not lose your insights. Your vitality will not be taken from you through inappropriate conversations.

<a id="p3"></a>Discretion is necessary for depth of understanding as well as for the development of discernment. Practice stillness in your meditation and practice awareness and discretion upon the hour. How much time would be saved and how much suffering would be prevented if people would practice this.
