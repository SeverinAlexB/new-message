# Step 12: Today I Will Not Try to Be Happy.

<a id="p1"></a>Being happy is the natural result of being aligned with Knowledge within yourself and of being close to those who are meant to be with you and close to that which you are meant to do. This is a deeper kind of happiness, a confirming kind of happiness, a happiness that resonates all through your life. It establishes a rightness with yourself, a sense of integrity and harmony despite whatever difficulties may be facing you in the moment. For if you are not right with yourself, then what pleasantries on the outside can allay the pain and the discomfort that this produces? And yet most people suffer under this duress, for they are not where they need to be, they are not with who they need to be with. They are often not engaged in the right activities.
And this produces a discomfort, a sense of wrongness that is very difficult to avoid.

<a id="p2"></a>Therefore, today do not try to be happy. Instead, look at all the activities that people do to try to be happy. Look at the expense of money, of time, of energy trying to be happy. Look at the rewards. What a high cost is being paid for such fleeting experiences. And look deeper at whether someone is really right with their own life. The more that people are not right with their life, the more that they have to engage in outside activities to try to allay their own suffering and discomfort, and the more desperate this becomes, and the greater the costs, and the more energy invested, and so forth. Desperately seeking relief, they are trying to be happy.

<a id="p3"></a>Today you do not need to try to be happy. In your practice in stillness and in your hourly practices in awareness and discernment, take another step towards bringing your life into true balance. This cannot all be done at once. It is a process with many steps and many important decisions along the way. But each step in this direction gives you a firm foundation. It is a gift that keeps giving itself within you. Then you find happiness in work and work in happiness. And the desperate attempt to escape the world and your condition will not be so prevailing, and you will find a greater comfort and a greater ease being in the world even as it is.

<a id="p4"></a>If you are to be a force for good in the world, then everything about your life must be in harmony. That does not mean that everything that happens to you is good. It does not mean that the world is in harmony around you. But it does mean that the Four Pillars of your life are in an essential balance.

<a id="p5"></a>Beyond your practice in stillness and your hourly practices, think about this today.
