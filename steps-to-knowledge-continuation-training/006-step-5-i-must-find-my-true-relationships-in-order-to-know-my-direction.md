# Step 5: I Must Find my True Relationships in Order to Know my Direction.

<a id="p1"></a>A critical part of your development is finding those individuals who are destined to share your greater work in the world. Finding them, then, can preoccupy the early stages of your development. And in this it is often necessary to leave other involvements that do not represent this greater purpose and destiny. For some people this can take many years to accomplish. Yet until you are free to find your true companions, then you must engage in this work, in this effort.
And if you have been so fortunate to find even one of your true companions, then you have a new beginning in your life. And your development and their development becomes really essential. Here you become accountable to more than just yourself. For you now practice for others as well as for your own advancement.. Here failure means you fail them and your Spiritual
Family.

<a id="p2"></a>Receive this not with guilt but understand that it gives you great strength and commitment, for in reality this is the case. People believe that they are weak and so they look for other powers in the universe to guide them and to protect them. But here you are learning The Way of Knowledge, which is the way of true initiation. For this you will need strong companions. And they must become strong for you and you must become strong for them. You must become strong to find them. You must become strong to join with them. And you must become strong to advance with them. This is a gift.

<a id="p3"></a>Today spend time thinking about the meaning of this. Today's lesson holds the key to understanding human relationships, and a great deal can be revealed to you if you do spend the time to consider the meaning of today's practice.
Look upon the world without condemnation but with clear eyes. See that without a greater purpose, human relationships cannot fulfill their greater meaning and value. They can only be partial, and they are at the mercy of the conflicts of the mind of those who participate within them. And they are governed by the conditioning of the world and freedom and true self expression will not be possible within them. Understand this and you will understand your past and a great deal about what has brought you to this point.

<a id="p4"></a>Continue your one daily practice in stillness and your hourly practices. Think about your greater companions.
