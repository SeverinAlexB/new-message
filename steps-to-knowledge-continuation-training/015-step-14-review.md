# Step 14: Review

<a id="p1"></a>Here we have our first Review Practice in the STEPS TO KNOWLEDGE CONTINUATION
TRAINING. In our Reviews, it is important to focus on the Four Pillars of your life. This is
"checking in" so to speak in building the foundation that you need to build, each day, and which will serve you each day.

<a id="p2"></a>Today, review all the former lessons that we have given. Read through them, and if you have kept a journal, which we recommend, recall what happened to you on those days. See what has occurred. See where you were present and not present. See how things influenced you, and how you influenced them. Begin to see these kinds of relationships in action.

<a id="p3"></a>Also today in this Review, it is time to check in on the Four Pillars of your life, remembering that each Pillar is equally important, and though one may require more effort or attention at any given moment, they are all equally important—-like the four legs of a table, each must be strong.
None can be sacrificed for the others, and you cannot build one to the neglect of all the others, or nothing great can be given to you and nothing great can be realized.

<a id="p4"></a>Come then to your Pillar of Relationship in your practice today. We shall not practice stillness but we shall do this Four Pillars Review. Come then to the first Pillar, the Pillar of relationship, and ask within yourself, "Is there anything I need to know about any of my relationships?" Ask this question in such a way that only a Yes or a No can be an answer. If the answer is No, that is fine. If the answer is yes, seek out who that might be, what relationship. Maybe it is the relationship that you need to attend to at this moment. Ask yourself, "Is there something I need to know here?" Then open yourself to receive what that could be. This can only take a moment, but you must be very present.

<a id="p5"></a>Then go on to the Pillar of Career and Providership, the Pillar that deals with work, money and contribution in the world. And continue the same process, "Is there anything I need to know regarding my Career and Providership in the world?" And if something comes to mind, go to that, and ask yourself, "Is there something I need to know here?" Then open yourself to what that could be.

<a id="p6"></a>Then you go on to the Pillar of Health and you ask yourself, "Is there anything that I need to know about my health or about the health of those who are close to me?" And if something comes to mind, go to that and say, "Is there something I need to know here?" And if there is, open yourself to it. Face it. Receive it.

<a id="p7"></a>Then go on to your Pillar of Spiritual Advancement and ask yourself, "Is there something I need to know regarding my spiritual advancement?" And like with all of the other Pillars, if something comes to mind, you go to that and you ask, "Is there something I need to know here?"

<a id="p8"></a>This process of visiting your Four Pillars represents your primary practice for this Review. If the
Review takes more than one day, then do this practice on each day. We shall continue this Four
Pillars Review in each of your Review Practices in the CONTINUATION TRAINING. This then

<a id="p9"></a>will give you a focus in your life, to maintain an awareness in these four fundamental areas of activity. And we begin to build, brick by brick, stone by stone, a foundation that the world cannot shake or undermine.

<a id="p10"></a>In addition today, practice your hourly practices until they become automatic, until your mind becomes present in every situation that you are in. This is the goal towards which we are working every day.
