# Step 9: The Greater Community is in the World.

<a id="p1"></a>Forces from the Greater Community are in the world. This can be known. This can be felt.
Perhaps you are feeling it already or have felt it in the past. The world's emergence into the
Greater Community is well underway, but who can respond to it? Who can understand what it means or what its implications are? In developing your relationship with the world, then, and your relationship with Knowledge, you must come to understand the condition of the world that you have been sent to serve. Knowledge understands this, but the personal mind, having been built upon fear and preference, cannot see this yet. Therefore, what is obvious escapes the attention of nearly everyone, and what is imaginary seems to be highly agreed upon. At this point, it is important to remember that the Greater Community forces are in the world, and you must come to learn what this means for you.

<a id="p2"></a>In your practice in stillness, let this awareness naturally emerge, which it naturally will over time. In your hourly practices, try to be attentive to where you are and to what is happening. And today take some time to consider today's lesson. Perhaps it does not conform with your ideas.
Perhaps it does. Perhaps you have experienced it directly. Perhaps you have not. But if you become attentive, you can realize that the condition of your world has changed, and part of this change is the presence of other forms of intelligent life here. Become still and over time this will become very clear to you.
