# Step 36: Nasi Novare Coram

<a id="p1"></a>Today we practice the great invocation that recognizes the presence of the Unseen Ones in your life. It is a remembrance of the Greater Reality that you have come from and the Greater Reality that you are to represent here in the world. This is a Reality not only of your world but of the
Greater Community as a whole. For the presence of the Unseen Ones can be felt anywhere in the universe because their presence is universal.

<a id="p2"></a>Spend time today thinking about this—-the reality of the Unseen Ones— and think of this invocation as a calling for their help and their protection. When you are afraid or feel endangered and cannot rid this fear from your mind, call for their help and their protection. Ask that they surround you and protect you. Call them forth with Nasi Novare Coram and open yourself to receive them. They will come to you if you call them sincerely. And they will help you if you can open yourself to them. In this way, you will have the greater strength that you need, for you represent the strength of Heaven now, and not merely the strength of one individual.

<a id="p3"></a>Practice in stillness today and exercise discernment and awareness upon the hour. And upon the hour complete each remembrance with the invocation, Nasi Novare Coram.
