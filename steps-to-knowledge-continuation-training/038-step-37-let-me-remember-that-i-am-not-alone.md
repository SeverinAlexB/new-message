# Step 37: Let me Remember That I Am Not Alone

<a id="p1"></a>In the world you seem to be alone, as does everyone else. Loneliness seems to be your state here, and only if you are fortunate do you find someone else to share your life with in a meaningful way. Certainly, this reality is in contrast to your Ancient Home, where everyone is included and everything is complete. That is why it takes so long for a human being to get used to being in a world like this because it is not a natural state. This gives evidence to your mission and your purpose here, for you bring your Ancient Home into the world as you learn The Ways of
Knowledge and as you allow Knowledge to express itself through you.

<a id="p2"></a>Today, then, be a vehicle for Knowledge and be a representative of Knowledge to the best of your ability by remembering that you are not alone. Upon the hour, as you practice awareness and discernment, also remind yourself that you are not alone today, that your Spiritual Family goes with you, and that there are others in the world who are working in tandem with you, many of whom you will meet in the course of your lifetime if you are able to proceed in your preparation.

<a id="p3"></a>Bring this awareness into the world, where it has been lost. Remind yourself and demonstrate to yourself that you are included in a Greater Plan and a greater promise for humanity. This is a plan and a promise from the Creator so that you may grow and advance and become united and free in the Greater Community.
