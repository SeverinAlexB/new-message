# Step 83: Let Peace Be with me Today.

<a id="p1"></a>There is peace in knowing that you are on the right tack. Even if you have much work to do.
There is peace in knowing that your life is coming into right order within yourself. There is peace in confirming that your relationship with Knowledge is being honored and furthered.
There is peace in knowing that resolution is coming in conflicts and relationships around you.

<a id="p2"></a>When you attend to what you know and follow what you know, this gives you inner confirmation. This heals the division within yourself and brings real restitution to your experience. As you advance as a student of Knowledge, your problems will be more and more circumstantial and not personal in nature. You will encounter difficult thresholds along the way, but increasingly you will not be the problem. The Wise are more concerned with negotiating circumstances than they are with negotiating internal difficulties. This is an unparalleled freedom and accomplishment.

<a id="p3"></a>As you speak to others today, you will hear how much they do not have this peace. You will see that they are not yet right with themselves and how much work remains for this to be accomplished. Therefore, take time today to experience this peace in your meditation in stillness and in your hourly practice. Look with gratitude upon your life, for it is moving. You are escaping the past You are leaving the past behind. You are moving into a new life and a new awareness and understanding. The power is in your life, and Grace is abiding with you as you are learning to abide with it
