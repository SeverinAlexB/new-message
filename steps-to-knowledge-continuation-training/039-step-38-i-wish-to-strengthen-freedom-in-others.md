# Step 38: I Wish to Strengthen Freedom in Others.

<a id="p1"></a>You cannot convince other people to be free, but you can demonstrate freedom to them and provide a contrast to them that is memorable and that they can recognize and understand. When you become free of social conditioning, when you become free in the Mental Environment, when you become liberated from the shadows of your own past, you can demonstrate a spontaneity and an enthusiasm in life that are remarkably refreshing to others.

<a id="p2"></a>This is a practical demonstration of freedom. It is a demonstration that is needed in the world. It is a demonstration that you give every time that you practice stillness wholeheartedly and with a sense of dedication. It is a demonstration of freedom that you practice when you stop what you are doing upon the hour to check yourself and your environment and to remind yourself that
Knowledge is with you and to listen for Knowledge if that is needed. This is a remarkable act of liberation, for you are setting yourself apart from all the forces that govern everyone here, stepping outside the world’s influence, pulling yourself out of its current to exercise a greater awareness and discernment, which are yours to exercise.

<a id="p3"></a>Remember, then, today that you practice for everyone and that your demonstrations of freedom, however small and seemingly insignificant, can hold great value to others and can have a powerful and lasting effect upon their lives.
