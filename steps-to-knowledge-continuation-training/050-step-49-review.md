# Step 49: Review

<a id="p1"></a>Read the past six lessons and consider their meaning and their value. See what influence they have had upon your life. Try to delve deeper into the message that they are giving you and the foundation that they are helping you to build for yourself.

<a id="p2"></a>Then turn your attention to the Four Pillars of your life. Again review your Four Pillars, listening for Knowledge—-listening to hear if something needs your attention, listening with deeper ears, without preference, without fear, even without hope. The problem with hope is that it is a cover for fear. The only real hope that you have is that Knowledge is with you and that you cannot lose
Knowledge. That is true, but beyond that, hope is simply an adaptation to the existence of fear.

<a id="p3"></a>Therefore, come to your practices with an open mind and an open heart. You are here to learn—- not to demand, not to defend, not to protect, not to negotiate, but to receive and to learn so that you may be able to give.
