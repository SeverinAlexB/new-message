# Step 55: There Are Forces in the World Which I Must Comprehend.

<a id="p1"></a>There are forces that affect people in the world that you must comprehend. You must gain an understanding of why people all think the same, why they have the same attitudes, why they use the same words and phrases, why they have the same responses, responses that do not come from
Knowledge, why they hold to the same assumptions without question, why they follow one another blindly around, why they act mindlessly and foolishly and why they hurt themselves and other people.

<a id="p2"></a>If these forces were not in the world, the world would be a place of Knowledge and would be totally unrecognizable from what you see today. So clearly you must understand the forces that limit you and hinder you if you are to advance. Do not claim that they are beyond your scope or beyond your comprehension, for this is not true.

<a id="p3"></a>Therefore, today think about these things. Do not assign a cause to a person or an institution. Do not say, "Well, the government is this, and the government is that." There are greater forces. Yes, they can manifest through individuals, organizations, groups, authority figures and so forth. But really, what is this power? Why is it so effective and why do people succumb to it so easily?
How is it being used by those who wish to control others? And how does it control those people who are doing the controlling? Do not try to answer these questions, for this is an exploration.
The important issue here is that you are asking the questions, not that you are giving yourself the answers.

<a id="p4"></a>In your practice in stillness, let all questioning go. Practice upon the hour, using awareness and discernment. If you are advancing in this Curriculum, you will begin to feel and sense things that you only intermittently felt before. You will sense the presence of forces. You will feel influences within people and around people. You will see that there really are counter-productive forces in the world. They can act through people. They can act independently of people. This is why you feel good in certain places and not so good in others. This is why one place does not feel like every other place, why one person does not feel like every other person. For Knowledge to be so thwarted in the world, there must be significant forces opposing it. Today begin to explore what this could mean and where this could possibly come from.
