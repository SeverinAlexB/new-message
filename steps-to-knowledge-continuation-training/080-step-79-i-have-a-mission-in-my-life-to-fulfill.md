# Step 79: I Have a Mission in my Life to Fulfill.

<a id="p1"></a>This is most certainly true, but it exists so far outside the boundaries of personal concerns and preoccupations that most people simply cannot gain access to it in any way at all. They are so given to survival and gratification that they cannot see anything else. They are intoxicated with themselves and with those things that they use for their own self-avoidance. They can only be led by powers that are beyond themselves. They are helpless to resist.

<a id="p2"></a>Yet for you the idea of mission is something that is beginning to become real or you would not have progressed this far in the Curriculum. Though you are still a beginning student of
Knowledge, you are starting to have a foundation, and that is real. That is worth so much.

<a id="p3"></a>Remind yourself today that you have a mission in life to fulfill, but leave that mission undefined.
In your practice in stillness and in your hourly practice, exercise your authority by entering those practices with real determination. This is an expression of your mission, If you cannot prepare for it, you cannot receive it. Therefore, your mission now is to prepare so that you can receive.
