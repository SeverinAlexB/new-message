# Step 11: I Will Remember Those Who Have Given to Me.

<a id="p1"></a>In today’s practice in gratitude, recall those individuals who have given to you. Think about what they have given, and do not limit them only to those who seem to encourage you in a positive way, for even your adversaries have made you strong, have made you stand upon your own ground, have required you to establish your freedom and to secure it for yourself. Ask yourself,
"Who do I need to acknowledge today for serving me?" And you might be surprised as to who comes to mind. Whoever does come to mind, recognize a gift. What did they give you in enabling you to find yourself and your way in life? Here your supporters and your critics alike become valuable resources. In this, you will learn not to be offended by those who seem to criticize you today, for tomorrow you will recall them in such a way that you will appreciate the opportunity that they provided you to become stronger with Knowledge and closer to the grace that lives within you and all around you.

<a id="p2"></a>Beyond your practice in stillness and your hourly exercises in awareness and discernment, spend time considering the meaning of today’s lesson and receiving those who come to you as you think about it. The past must be a record of gratitude if it is to have any meaning or value at all, and today we learn to seek and to find that gratitude.
