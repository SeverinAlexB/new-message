# Step 26: Today I Will Learn of the Greater Community.

<a id="p1"></a>This day you can learn a little more about the Greater Community, and even more than that if you should choose to do so. Through the study in Greater Community Spirituality, we have given you a perspective. We have given you a vantage point from which to see. Gain this vantage point. Utilize this perspective and you will see what could not be seen otherwise.

<a id="p2"></a>The Greater Community is the bigger picture of your life. It is the larger context for you to understand who you are and why you are here. It takes the parameters of life beyond personal preference and anxiety and places you in the world, in relationship to the world, in relationship to others, and in relationship to the world’s evolution. This is part of the Revelation that you may receive today. And it will be a Revelation because it will liberate you from so many constraints that hurt you and belittle you and keep your mind in a state of darkness.

<a id="p3"></a>Your future is in the Greater Community. Your work is in the Greater Community. Your preparation is for the Greater Community. That which seems so far beyond your personal and immediate concerns is the very thing that gives meaning and direction to your life. For this is the time that humanity begins to recognize its opportunity and its predicament within the context of the Greater Community. And this is the time when people must learn to prepare and to become educated about life and reality and spirituality within the larger context of their lives.

<a id="p4"></a>Reinforce your desire and capacity for this today through your one long practice in stillness and through your hourly practices in awareness and discernment. And take time to consider today your relationship with the Greater Community. Seek to know more. Ask to know more. Try to penetrate the veil that keeps you from understanding life at a larger level.
