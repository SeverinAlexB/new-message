# Step 28: Review

<a id="p1"></a>Today review the prior six steps and your journal. Also today practice once again in your long practice period your Four Pillar Review. Come back to your Pillars with as little fear and preference as possible because you want to practice seeing and knowing. Do not try to protect anything. Come to your Relationships, your Career and Providership, Health and Spiritual
Advancement with as open a mind as possible. Ask yourself, "Which of my Pillars is weak right now? Which of my Pillars requires my attention? Where am I at risk? Where is my life jeopardized? Where can I be undermined?" Ask yourself these questions and pay special attention to any Pillar that stands out from all the rest.
