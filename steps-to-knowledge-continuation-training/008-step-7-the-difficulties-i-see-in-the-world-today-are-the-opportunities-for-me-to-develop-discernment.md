# Step 7: The Difficulties I See in the World Today Are the Opportunities for me to Develop Discernment.

<a id="p1"></a>It is important in learning The Way of Knowledge to be able to face anything and everything that comes across your path. That does not, however, mean that you give these things equal value or meaning. There is great conflict in the world. This cannot be denied. Yet it must be understood clearly. You cannot put a happy face on it. You cannot say that it is merely a projection of your own imagination. Do not call it unreal and go seek out those things that are pleasurable instead.
For in your relationship with the world, conflict is something that must be faced, and this will give you courage and self-determination if you can do this. The conflicts in the world give you the opportunity to develop true discernment - seeing what really is, knowing what really is, all without condemnation.

<a id="p2"></a>Look at the conflicts that cross your path today and take a moment and see them without judgment. Suspend your judgment so that you may see past what appears to be obvious at the surface and allow a deeper understanding to naturally and gradually come into being within your experience. Here we set aside all simple definitions and enter into the experience. Here we do not put labels on things. Begin to understand them.

<a id="p3"></a>In your practice in stillness, let your mind be free. In your hourly practices, apply the mind purposefully, meaningfully, and today look at conflict and see what you can see.
