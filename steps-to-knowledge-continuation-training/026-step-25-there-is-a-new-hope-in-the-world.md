# Step 25: There is a New Hope in the World.

<a id="p1"></a>Hope in the world is rekindled by those who become strong with Knowledge. Hope can fade away and can be reignited. It can seem to come and to go, depending on how people are swayed and what they choose for themselves. Hope rests with you. Because the Unseen Ones are here does not mean that there is hope, for without you, there would be no hope! For you and others like you are bringing a new hope into the world because you are learning The Way of
Knowledge and because you are learning to receive the gift of Knowledge. This brings a new hope into the world. Perhaps you cannot see this fully at this moment. Perhaps it seems beyond your understanding. But from our perspective, it is so very true and so very important.

<a id="p2"></a>The world’s emergence into the Greater Community speaks to this, for if no one were preparing for the Greater Community, if no one were learning The Greater Community Way of Knowledge or Greater Community Spirituality, well, then hope would seem to fade. And humanity’s destiny would seem to be utterly predictable. But because there is hope in the world, because there is hope in you and others like you who have received and are responding to a greater calling, then the destiny of humanity has greater promise, and the freedom of humanity may well yet be secured.

<a id="p3"></a>Consider this today and strengthen the reality of this through your practice in stillness and your hourly practices in discernment and awareness. Become the hope and all self-hatred will disappear from your mind.
