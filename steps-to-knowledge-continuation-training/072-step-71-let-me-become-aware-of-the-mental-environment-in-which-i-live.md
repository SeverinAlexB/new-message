# Step 71: Let me Become Aware of the Mental Environment in Which I Live.

<a id="p1"></a>You live in a Mental Environment as well as a physical one. The Mental Environment is filled with forces, thought forms and thought forces, generated by thinking beings everywhere. You have experienced this, and you do experience it every day. Walk into any house, any building, any place where people are congregated, and you will experience the Mental Environment of that place--an environment that is created through the predominating thoughts and interactions of people who dwell there.

<a id="p2"></a>Become sensitive to this environment. Feel it wherever you go. When you are out of doom, it dissipates, though places in genera] can have an overall feel to them, depending upon what has occurred their in the past and what the prevailing ideas are in that particular location.

<a id="p3"></a>But indoors particularly, and in close proximity to others, you will experience unique Mental
Environments. Some you will find agreeable. Some you will not find agreeable. The question to ask yourself is, "Where do I need to be?" Sometimes you need to be in a disagreeable place to understand it, to discern it and to learn further about the mechanisms in the Mental Environment.
These are forces that influence you every day and that on many occasions govern you completely. This is an essential part of your preparation for the Greater Community, where the emphasis on control of the Mental Environment has been developed to a far greater degree than is apparent in your world. Here you can practice everywhere you go because everywhere you go, there is a Mental Environment. With everyone you spend time with closely, you create a Mental
Environment

<a id="p4"></a>The question to ask yourself is, "Is this the right place far me to be?" If it is the right place, then you must begin to learn about where you are. How does it feel? What are the characteristics?
What are the prevailing notions, ideas or sentiments that exist there? What do they motivate in you? Do they encourage you? Do they discourage you? Do they make you feel stronger? Do they make you feel weaker? Do they dissipate your energy or do they charge your energy?

<a id="p5"></a>Beyond your practice in stillness today and your hourly practice, carry on this investigation and do not forget. This is a day of practice, a day to deepen your understanding and to gain new skill and authority in your life.
