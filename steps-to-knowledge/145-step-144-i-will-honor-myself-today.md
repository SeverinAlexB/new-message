# Step 144: I will honor myself today.

<a id="p1"></a>Honor yourself because of your Heritage, because of your destiny and because of your purpose. Honor yourself because life honors you. Honor yourself because God is honored in God’s Creation in you. This eclipses all the evaluations that you have made upon yourself. This is greater than any criticism that you have levied against yourself. This is greater than any pride that you have used to offset your pain.

<a id="p2"></a>In simplicity and humility remind yourself upon the hour to honor yourself. In your two deeper practices today, allow yourself to experience the presence of Knowledge, for this honors you and honors Knowledge as well. Honor yourself this day so that Knowledge may be honored, for in reality you are
Knowledge. This is your True Self, but it is a Self that you are only now beginning to reclaim.

<a id="p3"></a>Practice 144: Two 30-minute practice periods.
Hourly practice.
