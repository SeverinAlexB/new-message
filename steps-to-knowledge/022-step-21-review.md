# Step 21: Review

<a id="p1"></a>In your third Review, review all the lessons of the past week and the results of those lessons. Practice today not making any conclusions, but simply recognize the line of development and take note of the progress that you have made thus far. It is too early to make genuine conclusions, though it may be very tempting to do so. Beginning students are not in a position to judge their curriculum. This right must be earned and comes later if you wish for your judgments to have true effect and to be wise.

<a id="p2"></a>Therefore, in your one practice period, review the last section of practice and all that has been experienced thus far.

<a id="p3"></a>Practice 21: One 45-minute practice period.
