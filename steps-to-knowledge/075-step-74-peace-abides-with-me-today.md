# Step 74: Peace abides with me today.

<a id="p1"></a>Today peace abides with you. Abide with peace and receive its blessings. Come to peace with all that troubles you. Come with your heavy burden. Come not seeking answers. Come not seeking understanding. Come seeking its blessings. Peace cannot intervene into a life of conflict, but you can enter into a life of peace. You come to peace, which is waiting for you, and in this your burdens will be released.

<a id="p2"></a>In your two long practice periods today, practice, in stillness, receiving peace. Allow yourself to have this gift, and if any thought comes to dissuade you, remind yourself of your great worth—the worth of your Knowledge and the worth of your Self. Know now that you are willing to learn from your errors and that you need not identify with them but use them only as a valuable resource for your development, for such they can become for you.

<a id="p3"></a>Practice, then, receiving. Open a little further today.
Set all things aside that preoccupy you for later consideration if necessary. Peace abides with you today. Today abide with peace.

<a id="p4"></a>Practice 74: Two 30-minute practice periods.
