# Step 7: Review

<a id="p1"></a>In today’s two practice periods, review all that We have covered thus far, beginning with the first step and continuing on up to include the preceding day’s step. Then consider the entire sequence of the steps together. It is very important at this juncture that you not require that you have any conclusions, but that you ask questions and realize the extent to which you need true Knowledge. If you undertake this practice today sincerely, it will be most evident that you have this great need. You are vulnerable without your assumptions, but you are also in a position to receive truth and certainty in life.

<a id="p2"></a>Take two practice periods today, 30 minutes each, to consider these things.

<a id="p3"></a>Practice 7: Two 30-minute practice periods.
