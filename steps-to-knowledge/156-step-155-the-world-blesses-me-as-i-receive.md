# Step 155: The world blesses me as I receive.

<a id="p1"></a>You are learning now to receive. The world blesses you as you learn to receive, for Knowledge will flow into you as you become an open receptacle for
Knowledge. And you will draw that which is life into yourself, for life is always attracted to those who give.

<a id="p2"></a>Understand this in its full depth this day as you remind yourself upon the hour that life gives to you as you are still. In your two meditation practices, enter stillness once again and feel life being drawn into you. This is a natural attraction.
As your giving and stillness become greater, you will feel life being drawn into you, for you will in time become a source of nourishment for life.

<a id="p3"></a>Practice 155: T wo 30-minute practice periods.
Hourly practice.
