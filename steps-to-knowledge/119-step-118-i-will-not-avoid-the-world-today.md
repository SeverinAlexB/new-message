# Step 118: I will not avoid the world today.

<a id="p1"></a>There is no need to avoid the world because the world cannot dominate you when you are with
Knowledge. When you are with Knowledge, you are here to serve the world. The world, then, is not a prison anymore. It is not a continual source of discomfort and disappointment. It provides for you an opportunity to give and an opportunity to re-establish your true understanding. Do not seek refuge in spiritual things, for your purpose is to give to the world. Allow the world to be as it is, and your condemnation of it will not come back to haunt you. For without condemnation, there is only the opportunity to give. This will draw upon your
Knowledge, which will give of its own, and you will be the vehicle for its giving.

<a id="p2"></a>Think of this now. Allow yourself in your two practice periods to experience the presence of Knowledge in your life.
Demand nothing of it. Seek not to question it. Only allow yourself to experience it, for with this all that you seek returns to you naturally without your effort. Utilize your self-discipline only to direct your mind in the proper direction. Once it is so engaged, it will return to Knowledge of its own accord. For that is its destination, that is its love, that is its true companion and that is its true marriage in life.

<a id="p3"></a>Practice 118: Two 30-minute practice periods.
